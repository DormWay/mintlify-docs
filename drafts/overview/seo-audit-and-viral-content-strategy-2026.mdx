# SEO Audit & Viral Content Strategy

## Executive Summary

DormWay has a superior product (50+ features across web + iOS, multi-LMS integration at 6,138 universities via ICS feeds, AI syllabus analysis, schedule screenshot import, city/campus intelligence) but lacks the content marketing and SEO infrastructure that competitors like Turbo AI have deployed.

**Real Student Impact:** DormWay was built by Riley Crimmins, a current University of Michigan student facing syllabi chaos and weather unpredictability in Ann Arbor. When it's 15°F with freezing rain, students need more than just a calendar app—they need smart departure timing based on weather, campus traffic, and walking times.

## Viral Content Strategy

### Content Pillar 1: Multi-LMS Integration Magic
**Hook:** "Connect Your LMS in 30 Seconds (No IT Approval)"
- Target pain point: IT bureaucracy and broken integrations
- Demo: ICS feed workflow across Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard
- Appeal: Works with all 6 major platforms instantly

### Content Pillar 2: AI Syllabus Analysis
**Hook:** "I Emailed My Syllabus to AI and It Found Every Deadline"
- Target pain point: Hidden syllabus requirements and grading tricks
- Demo: Email to `syllabus@dormway.app` → AI extracts deadlines + gotchas
- Appeal: Instant gratification + AI wow factor

### Content Pillar 3: Schedule Screenshot Import
**Hook:** "I Took a Photo of My Schedule and AI Did the Rest"
- Target pain point: Manual calendar entry from paper/PDF schedules
- Demo: Photo upload → AI extraction → Full digital calendar
- Appeal: Ultimate convenience, no typing required

### Content Pillar 4: Weather + Transit Intelligence
**Hook:** "Should I Walk or Take the Bus? My App Tells Me."
- Target pain point: Campus mobility decisions in any weather
- Demo: Morning notification with weather-aware travel recommendations
- Appeal: Works across all climates (Michigan cold, Florida heat, California wildfires)

### Content Pillar 5: iOS Home Screen Widgets
**Hook:** "Your Schedule Lives on Your iPhone Home Screen"
- Target pain point: Opening multiple apps to see what's next
- Demo: 6 widget types showing real-time schedule data
- Appeal: Always-on visibility, aesthetic home screen customization
