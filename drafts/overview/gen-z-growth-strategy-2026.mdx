# Gen Z Growth Strategy 2026

## Executive Summary

DormWay has product-market fit. The growth challenge is amplification, not the product itself. Gen Z adopts apps through social proof, campus density, and identity expression — not traditional marketing.

**Three strategic pillars:**
- **Density over breadth** — Deep campus penetration before expansion
- **Social proof engine** — Turn usage into shareable campus identity
- **Program acceleration** — Labs, Creators, Ambassadors as distribution channels

**Budget:** $0–5K/month using commission-based programs and engineering resources.

## Current Website Analysis

### Homepage Growth Assets
- "College, decluttered" — clean, memorable positioning
- Interactive upload widget with zero friction
- School logos (Michigan, UCLA, Northwestern, Johns Hopkins, Harvard)
- "2M+ deadlines tracked · 100% free for students" credibility markers
- Calendar preview visualization
- `syllabus@dormway.app` frictionless entry point

### The Story Page — Hidden Growth Asset
The `/about` page contains DormWay's best content: the origin story of Riley buying 1,800 elevator LCDs for $600 on Twitch, leading to the smart mirror prototype and eventual GPS for college concept. This narrative is perfect for TikTok content but buried in navigation.

### Programs Page Structure
Three tracks with self-selection:
- **Labs** — Task-based earning ($5 per 24-hour turnaround)
- **Creators** — $25 per accepted post plus referral bonuses
- **Ambassadors** — $5 onboard + $2 D7 + $3 W4 per referral, plus milestone bonuses

## Growth Initiatives

### Campus Density Strategy
Focus on 10 pilot campuses for 25%+ penetration before expanding:
- Michigan, UCLA, Michigan State, UT Austin, Ohio State
- Indiana, UMass Amherst, USC, NYU, Georgia Tech

**Mechanics:** Track campus adoption percentage. At 5%: trigger peer notifications. At 10%: surface leaderboards. At 25%: incoming freshmen have existing peer network.

### Ambassador Program Structure
**Current payout:** $10 max per referral ($5 onboard + $2 D7 + $3 W4)
**Milestone bonuses:** +$50 at 50 referrals, +$100 at 100, +$200 at 150

**Three ambassador tiers:**
1. **Tier 1:** Any student — base commission
2. **Tier 2:** Org leaders or 2K+ campus following — commission + early access + Riley DM access  
3. **Tier 3:** 10K+ following — commission + monthly calls + product input

### Creator Pod System
Creators post on their own channels with tracked referral links. Organized into campus pods of 3–5 creators with one pod lead for coordination.

**Pay structure:**
- $25 per accepted post (quality gated, no view minimum)
- Standard referral bonuses ($5/$2/$3 per user)
- Same milestone structure as ambassadors

### Riley's Distribution Activation
Riley has 100K+ followers across TikTok, Twitch, Snap, Instagram.

**Content series planned:**
- "The $600 elevator screen story" (4-part TikTok series)
- Live semester setup demos
- "What Canvas won't show you" policy extraction demos
- Ace AI demonstration videos

## Viral Mechanics

### #DormWayOrganized Challenge
Students post semester setup content showing DormWay dashboard. Weekly $100 Amazon prizes, $500 for most viral semester post.

**Timing:** March launch, major August move-in week push.

### Crews Pre-Launch Seeding
For Q2 2026 Crews launch:
- Identify 50 groups (Greek chapters, sports teams, honor societies)
- "Crews Beta" early access requiring DormWay sign-ups
- Target 500 active crews at launch

### Move-In Week Strategy
Commission-based ambassadors table at dorm check-ins with live 60-second setup demos. No upfront costs — ambassador earns via referrals.
