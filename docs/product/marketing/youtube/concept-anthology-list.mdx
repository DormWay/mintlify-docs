# DormWay Content Anthology: The 6 Core Cinematic Concepts

These concepts are designed as 60-90 second "Genre Satire" shorts. They are **cinematic**, **funny but clever**, and **highly visual**.

---

## 1. "The Academic Heist" (Genre: Heist / Mission Impossible)
**Concept:** A high-stakes tactical "extraction" of a deadline buried in a 40-page PDF.
**The Scene:** Night, blue-toned lighting, headsets, multiple monitors in a dark dorm room. 
**The Action:** A student "sweatily" scans a document. "I can't find it! The Late Policy is encrypted!"
**The Payoff:** The leader slides a phone across: **DormWay Syllabus AI** highlights the exact "Gotcha" (Late Penalty: 10% per hour). 
**DormWay Flex:** It positions the **Syllabus Parser** as "Espionage-level" intelligence.

## 2. "The Prophet of the Blue Bus" (Genre: Philosophical Sci-Fi / Stalker-esque)
**Concept:** A student who "knows" the bus schedule with supernatural precision in a blizzard.
**The Scene:** A brutal Ann Arbor winter morning. 5 miserable students huddled. 1 calm student holding hot coffee, no gloves, staring into the distance. 
**The Action:** Student asks: "Is it even coming?" Calm student: "14 seconds. Door three." 
**The Payoff:** Bus appears through the snow. Stops *exactly* in front of him. Door three opens *exactly* where he is. 
**DormWay Flex:** Showcases **City Intelligence (Bus GPS/Door Alignment)** as "Prophecy."

## 3. "The Morning Symmetry" (Genre: Wes Anderson / Minimalist)
**Concept:** A student living a life of "Perfect Order" through the DayPlan.
**The Scene:** Symmetrical, centered shots, pastel colors, ASMR sound design. Pencil sharpening, coaster placement.
**The Action:** Student taps "Sync" on the **DormWay DayPlan**. Fast, mechanical cuts of him walking through campus in perfect sync with the world.
**The Payoff:** Every door opens exactly as he reaches it. The bus arrives exactly as he steps out. The timing is unsettlingly perfect.
**DormWay Flex:** Visual representation of "Zero Friction" through **DayPlan Generation**.

## 4. "The Oracle of Ann Arbor" (Genre: Neo-Noir / Mystery)
**Concept:** A student who "knows the future" of his own workload.
**The Scene:** Library at 3:00 AM. Rain outside. Shadows, high-contrast lighting (orange/yellow glow). 
**The Action:** He looks at the **Workload Forecast** graph on a glowing monitor. It shows a massive "Peak" coming in 48 hours. 
**The Payoff:** He is calm. He packs his bag, walks past 5 panicking students, and disappears into the rain. He has the "Intel" they don't.
**DormWay Flex:** Positions the **Workload Forecast** as "Knowing the Future."

## 5. "The Academic Forensic Team" (Genre: Procedural / Mindhunter)
**Concept:** A "Whistleblower" style investigation into a student's "failure."
**The Scene:** Trashed dorm room. Investigators in suits with magnifying glasses.
**The Action:** Analyzing a syllabus. "He missed the 5:00 PM cutoff... but the AI found the 'Draft Status' loophole."
**The Payoff:** They find a second student calmly eating a sandwich. "He had the **DormWay Syllabus Intelligence**. He knew about the loophole."
**DormWay Flex:** Highlights the **"Gotcha Detection"** of the AI.

## 6. "The Merger" (Genre: Sorkin Walk-and-Talk / Corporate Satire)
**Concept:** Two students treat their "Semester" like a Fortune 500 company.
**The Scene:** Fast-paced walk-and-talk through the U-M Diag. Suits, briefcases (maybe).
**The Action:** Talking about "Divesting from Chem 210" because the "Workload-to-GPA yield" is at an all-time low.
**The Payoff:** They check the **Grade Calculator** and "Sign" a deal (exchange notes) like it's the NYSE.
**DormWay Flex:** Makes the **Grade Calculator** and **Workload Forecast** look like "Business Intelligence."
