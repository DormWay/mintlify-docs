# DormWay YouTube Content Strategy: The Genre Anthology

## 1. Core Philosophy: Cine-SaaS
Instead of traditional "vlogging" or "tutorials," DormWay's YouTube strategy leans into high-end **Genre Satire**. We treat mundane student problems (syllabi, weather, transit, deadlines) as high-stakes cinematic conflicts (heists, thrillers, noirs).

The goal is to position DormWay as the **"Invisible Hand"**—the tool that resolves the plot without being the center of attention.

## 2. Why This Works for the Founder
- **Low Stress:** No "hosting" required. The camera captures the story, not the vlogger.
- **High Signal:** Showcases film student expertise (lighting, grading, directing) which builds "Founder-Moat."
- **Scalable:** One-day shoots on the U-M campus using existing pro gear.
- **Evergreen:** These aren't "trend" videos; they are "Brand Assets" that look good for years.
- **VC Magnet:** Demonstrates high-level brand thinking and "Cool Factor" for the Seed Round.

## 3. Brand Pillars
- **Clever Humor:** The comedy comes from the *absurdity of the stakes*, not from being "dumb."
- **Premium Aesthetic:** Use high-end gear (Red/Sony/Alexa style) to make a dorm room look like a bunker.
- **Technical Easter Eggs:** Hide actual snippets of the `dormway-platform` code in the background of shots to prove engineering depth.
- **Sonic Branding:** Use a consistent, high-quality "DormWay Sync" sound as a recurring audio cue (Leitmotif).

## 4. Key Metrics (Non-Vanity)
- **Brand Sentiment:** Qualitative comments on production value.
- **Technical Pauses:** Viewers pausing to read the hidden code/UI details.
- **Campus Density:** Mentions of "The Bus Prophet" or "The Heist" in Ann Arbor student circles.
- **Seed Round Inbound:** Interest from VCs who see the "Cultural Movement" potential.
