# Production Guide: Low-Stress High-Art Shorts

This guide is for a **Filmmaker-Founder** with pro gear but limited time.

## 1. The "Single-Day Shoot" Workflow
- **Batching:** Shoot 2-3 shorts in a single 4-hour window on the U-M campus or in your dorm.
- **Minimal Cast:** Use yourself (no dialogue) or 1-2 film student friends.
- **No Hosting:** Never look at the camera. No "vlogger" intro/outro.
- **Natural Sound:** Use high-quality ambient sounds (bus brakes, library whispers, snow crunch) + the **DormWay Notification sound** as the only recurring audio cue.

## 2. Equipment Strategy (Use What You Have)
- **Camera:** Use your high-end gear (Red/Sony/Alexa style). Focus on shallow depth of field for "Dorm Room" shots to make them look like "Bunkers."
- **Lighting:** One key light + one practical (e.g., a glowing phone or monitor). 
- **Lenses:** Focus on macro shots (phone screen, widgets, Syllabus text) and wide symmetrical shots (Wes Anderson style).

## 3. Post-Production (The "Clever" Edit)
- **Color Grading:** Use distinct LUTs for different genres (Blue for Heist, Yellow/Orange for Noir, Pastel for Symmetrical).
- **Speed Ramping:** Use fast cuts for "Tactical" shots and slow, lingering shots for "Oracle/Noir" pieces.
- **Easter Eggs:** Overlay 50% opacity code snippets in the background or show them on secondary monitors in the frame.

## 4. Branding Checklist
- [ ] **Sonic Branding:** High-quality "DormWay Sync" sound.
- [ ] **Feature Hero:** One clear DormWay widget or feature per video.
- [ ] **Founder Signature:** No face-to-camera, but use your silhouette or hands to maintain "Mystery."
- [ ] **The "Clever" Gag:** The high-stakes film resolves with a simple tap on the app.

## 5. Low-Stress Consistency
- **Goal:** One 60-second short every 30 days.
- **No "Algorithm" Chasing:** Post to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok simultaneously.
- **Quality over Quantity:** If you only make 6 of these a year, they should be "Museum Quality" brand assets.
