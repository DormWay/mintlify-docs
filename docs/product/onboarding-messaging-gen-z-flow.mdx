# DormWay Onboarding Messaging (Gen Z) - Proposed Flow

## Purpose
This document captures a proposed onboarding messaging flow for Gen Z college students. It is designed to align with DormWay goals: fast time-to-value, proof of success, and lightweight habit formation. The content is written in a neutral tone for future product, growth, and engineering review.

## North Star
Email is not the onboarding experience. Email is the "receipt + rescue + weekly preview" channel.

The flow should:
1. Get users to first value quickly (deadlines extracted, schedule consolidated).
2. Prove it worked (specific outcomes, not feature claims).
3. Build a light habit (one useful weekly preview).

All other programs (referrals, Labs, long feature lists) come after a user has had a clear win.

## Global Rules

### Send Cap
- Max 1 email per 24 hours unless it is a system-result email (e.g., parse success/failure).
- Avoid stacking emails on Day 1.

### Timing
- Do not send outside 9am to 9pm local time.
- Prefer late afternoon/early evening for nudges.
- Prefer Sunday evening for weekly preview.

### Tone
- Be honest and direct: "This is automated, but replies go to me."
- Keep copy short and scannable.
- One CTA per email.

### Content Rules
- One primary action per email.
- Prefer proof over promise (e.g., "We pulled 18 deadlines" vs. "Your semester command center").

## Behavioral Milestones (Event-Based)
Emails should be triggered by behavioral milestones rather than time-only sequences.

- Activation (primary): at least one syllabus successfully parsed.
- Activation (secondary): schedule imported OR Canvas connected.
- Habit: user has upcoming deadlines and opens the app at least weekly.

If these events are not available, adding them is recommended; the flow becomes significantly cleaner.

## Phase 0: Transactional (Only if Required)

**Email: Verify / magic link / account created**
- Functional only; no marketing.
- Skip if already handled in-app.

## Phase 1: Welcome (Day 0 - Single Email)

**Trigger:** `onboarding_completed` (or signup completed)

**Email 1: Welcome + single next step**
- Subject options:
  - "You're in. Upload one syllabus"
  - "Quick setup: 1 syllabus"
- Body: 2-3 lines max.
- CTA: Upload a syllabus.
- Add: "This is automated, but replies go to me."

**Hard rule:** Do not include referrals, Labs, App Store launch, feature grids, or multi-step checklists.

**Branch skip:** If syllabus uploaded within X minutes, skip to Phase 2 results email.

## Phase 2: Results (Highest ROI Emails)

**Email 2A: Syllabus parsed successfully (Immediate)**
- **Trigger:** `syllabus_parsed_success`
- Subject: "Pulled 18 deadlines from [Course]"
- Content: "We found X deadlines, Y exams, Z key dates."
- CTA: Review / confirm.
- Optional secondary: Upload another syllabus.

**Email 2B: Syllabus parse failed (Immediate)**
- **Trigger:** `syllabus_parsed_failed`
- Subject: "Couldn't read that syllabus (quick fix)"
- Content: 2-3 likely fixes (PDF export, higher-res image, include first pages, etc.).
- CTA: Re-upload.
- Offer: "Reply if you want me to take a look."

## Phase 3: "Complete Setup" Nudges (Only When Missing)

**Nudge A: No syllabus after 24 hours**
- **Trigger:** `onboarding_completed`
- **Condition:** no syllabus upload after 24h.
- One nudge only (or max two, spaced 24h and 72h).
- Subject: "Want DormWay to pull your deadlines?"
- CTA: Upload syllabus.

**Results Email: Schedule imported**
- **Trigger:** `schedule_parsed_success`
- Subject: "Your schedule is in"
- Content: one sentence confirmation.
- CTA: View schedule.

**Nudge B: Schedule missing (after syllabus success)**
- **Trigger:** `syllabus_parsed_success`
- **Condition:** no schedule after 48-72h.
- Subject: "One more thing: add your schedule"
- CTA: Upload schedule (screenshot/PDF/ICS).

**Canvas email (only if connected)**
- **Trigger:** `canvas_connected`
- Subject: "Canvas synced"
- Content: "Imported X assignments."
- CTA: Review.

**Canvas nudge (optional):** only if school uses Canvas and user is active.

## Phase 4: Week 1 Habit (Weekly Preview Only if Personalized)

**Sunday Preview**
- **Trigger:** weekly schedule job or Sunday 6-9pm
- **Condition:** user has at least one upcoming deadline/event
- Subject: "Your week, in 30 seconds"
- Content: top 3 upcoming deadlines + one heavy day warning (if applicable)
- CTA: Open week view

If personalization is not available, skip this email.

## Phase 5: Feedback (Early Product)

**Trigger:** user hits activation milestone (e.g., 2 syllabi parsed OR 3 sessions OR first week completed)

**Email: One question**
- Subject: "Quick question?"
- Content: one sentence context + one question (what was confusing, what should be fixed)
- CTA: reply (no links needed)

## Phase 6: Growth and Programs (Only After Wins)

**Referral / Ambassador**
- **Trigger:** clear success (e.g., 2+ syllabi + Sunday preview opened OR 7-day active)
- One clean invite.
- Lead with "If this helped," not "Earn money."

**Labs / paid work**
- **Trigger:** opted-in, visited Labs page, or power user segment.
- Not part of default nurture.

## Phase 7: Win-Back (Dormant Users)

- **7 days inactive:** one email (value-based or support-based).
- **21-30 days inactive:** one final check-in, then stop.

## Expected User Experience (Target Volume)
A typical engaged user should receive:
- 1 welcome email
- 1-2 results emails (syllabus parsed, schedule synced, Canvas synced)
- 1 weekly preview
- possibly 1 feedback ask

Target: ~2-5 emails in the first two weeks, only tied to user behavior.
