# DormWay GTM Playbook (Spring 2026)

Date: 2026-02-22
Owner: Riley
Scope: Student growth (PLG) + campus density + lifecycle retention

## 1. Objective
Build a repeatable growth system that increases Weekly First-Action Users (WFAU) without breaking trust or brand voice.

Primary metric:
- WFAU = new users who complete first action in 7 days.
- First action definition: syllabus uploaded, schedule imported, or Canvas connected.

Required retention lenses:
- D7 retention (first weekly cycle)
- D14 retention (second weekly cycle)
- W4 retention (month-scale durability)

Secondary metrics:
- Signup -> first action conversion
- Time-to-first-value (minutes from signup to first action)
- Ambassador-sourced WFAU
- Campus density (% active users in top target campuses)
- Referral-assisted first actions

## 2. Non-Negotiables
- Lead with outcomes, not AI mechanics.
- Keep language concrete and expectation-safe; avoid hype and ambiguous promises.
- Public student promise: "Free for students (core)."
- Public monetization clarity: paid plans are for org/admin and institutional pilots.
- Keep trust boundary visible: "We use AI for dates, not essays."
- Do not scale any channel until landing funnel instrumentation is decision-grade.

## 3. Strategic Decisions (Locked)
1. Engine hierarchy:
- Primary engine: campus distribution + in-product sharing loop.
- Secondary engine: short-form content as feeder into campus loop.
- Support engine: lifecycle messaging to lock habit.

2. Campus scope:
- Start with 5-8 target campuses.
- Expand only after density and retention thresholds are met.

3. Expansion gates (must all pass):
- At least 2 campuses show stable WFAU and non-degrading D14/W4.
- Referral/share flow contributes measurable first actions.
- Tracking is reliable by source and campus.

4. Monetization sequencing:
- Keep student acquisition and activation fully free and no-credit-card.
- Run near-term paid tests through org scheduling pass and institutional pilots.
- Do not message student premium plans in primary student flows.

## 4. Growth Model (3 Engines)

### Engine A: Campus Density (Primary)
Goal: concentrated adoption per campus, not shallow national spread.

Core loop:
1. Ambassador/officer shares targeted invite.
2. Friend completes first action quickly.
3. Student sees immediate timeline value.
4. Student shares with classmate/friend.
5. Campus-level density compounds.

Priority assets:
- Ambassador playbook + scripts focused on setup help.
- Campus scorecard (activation + D14/W4 + density).
- Officer-first outreach for org-level adoption.

### Engine B: Student PLG + Share (Secondary)
Goal: get first value fast, then turn value into sharing.

Core loop:
1. Student sees pain-led message.
2. Student uploads syllabus or connects Canvas.
3. DormWay returns immediate timeline value.
4. Student shares "week at a glance" with referral deep link.

Priority assets:
- Landing hero + first-action clarity.
- Shareable post-first-value artifact.
- Onboarding flow with one primary action.

### Engine C: Lifecycle (Retention)
Goal: behavior-triggered nudges that produce habit without spam.

Core loop:
1. Setup event happens (or does not happen).
2. Triggered message gives one next action.
3. Student returns and completes action.
4. Sunday preview reinforces weekly planning habit.

Priority assets:
- Sunday preview + one due-soon nudge.
- Event-triggered flows for incomplete setup.
- Frequency cap and user controls.

## 5. 90-Day Scenario Plan

### Scenario A (Weeks 1-3): Measurement-Ready
Definition:
- Landing upload events are live and trusted.
- Cohort reporting works by source and campus.
- D7/D14/W4 retention reporting exists.

Outcome:
- Keep/kill decisions become reliable.

### Scenario B (Weeks 4-8): Repeatable Campus Loop
Definition:
- At least 2 campuses show stable activation and non-degrading D14/W4.
- Referral/share contributes measurable first actions.

Outcome:
- Expand to additional campuses with similar profile.

### Scenario C (Weeks 8-12): Scale-Ready
Definition:
- 3-4 new campuses can repeat the same activation/retention pattern.
- No trust regressions in messaging or monetization clarity.

Outcome:
- Controlled expansion toward the 10-20 campus plan.

## 6. Weekly Operating Cadence

Monday (45 min): Growth planning
- Review KPI movement by source and campus.
- Pick 3 bets max (one per engine).
- Define owner + success threshold + decision deadline.

Tuesday-Thursday: Execution
- Ship assets/copy/tests.
- Monitor leading indicators daily.
- Kill obvious losers early.

Friday (45 min): Scoreboard + decisions
- Report WFAU, activation, D7/D14/W4 by source and campus.
- Keep/kill/iterate decisions.
- Lock next week's focus.

Friday (15 min): Trust QA ritual
- Review newly shipped quantified claims.
- Review "how we make money" copy.
- Review any AI references for boundary compliance.

## 7. KPI Definitions (Canonical)
- WFAU (7-day window): new users with >=1 first action event in first 7 days.
- Activation rate: WFAU / new signups.
- D7 retention: active on day 7 / activated on day 0.
- D14 retention: active on day 14 / activated on day 0.
- W4 retention: active in week 4 / activated in week 0.
- Time-to-first-value: median minutes from signup to first action.
- Ambassador conversion: ambassador-referred signups -> first action.

Active (retention) definition:
- A meaningful session with a tracked planning action (not just app open).

## 8. Event Instrumentation Checklist

Existing useful events:
- `homepage_viewed`
- `onboarding_started`
- `onboarding_completed`
- `syllabus_uploaded`
- `syllabus_parsed`
- `quickstart_modal_upload_started`
- `quickstart_modal_upload_completed`
- `dashboard_quickstart_upload_started`
- `dashboard_quickstart_upload_completed`
- `setup_widget_syllabus_uploaded`
- `referral_completed`

Priority 0 gap to close now:
- Landing uploader (`useMultiFileUpload`) uploads files but does not emit reliable start/completion tracking.

Required patch:
- Emit `landing_upload_started` on submit.
- Emit `landing_upload_completed` on completion with:
  - `source = landing_bootstrap`
  - `upload_method = drag_drop | file_picker | email`
  - `file_count`
  - `syllabus_count`
  - `schedule_count`
  - `other_count`
  - `detected_school`
  - `success_count`
  - `failure_count`
  - `error_reason` (if any)

Scale gate:
- No scaled channel experiments until landing upload attribution is verified in dashboards.

## 9. Channel Mix and Targets

Campus distribution (primary):
- Output: active ambassadors and officer setups in 5-8 campuses.
- KPI: WFAU, activation, D14/W4 quality by campus.

Short-form (secondary feeder):
- Output: 3-4 posts/week with single CTA into setup flow.
- KPI: source-tagged signups and first actions, not views/likes.

Lifecycle (support retention):
- Output: Sunday preview + one due-soon nudge + setup recovery flows.
- KPI: Monday/Tuesday actives, D14/W4 uplift in exposed cohorts.

SEO campus pages (support):
- Output: only where campus-specific setup guidance is useful.
- KPI: page -> signup -> first action at parity with top sources.

## 10. Messaging Matrix by Funnel Stage

Awareness:
- "11:48 PM and you still don't know what's due tomorrow?"
- "Never wonder what's due."

Consideration:
- "Upload a syllabus or connect Canvas. We build your timeline."
- "Free for students. No credit card."

Activation:
- "Upload one syllabus to preview your week."
- "Read-only access. Revoke anytime."

Retention:
- "Your week in 30 seconds."
- "What changed since last week?"

Referral:
- "If this saved you once, share it with one friend."
- "Send your week-at-a-glance link."

## 12. Risk Register
- Risk: monetization contradiction across surfaces.
  - Mitigation: single public monetization narrative and Trust QA ritual.

- Risk: expectation breaks from feature overclaims.
  - Mitigation: copy QA guardrails and manual-sync-safe language.

- Risk: false channel winners due to missing attribution.
  - Mitigation: Priority 0 instrumentation gate before scale.

- Risk: channel sprawl across too many campuses.
  - Mitigation: 5-8 campus focus and explicit expansion criteria.

Week 1:
- Lock monetization narrative and copy guardrails.
- Patch landing upload instrumentation.
- Establish baseline dashboard for activation + D7/D14/W4 by source/campus.
- Launch copy correction for grade sync language.
- Run first Sunday preview with control cohort.

Week 2:
- Review baseline + confidence intervals.
- Keep/kill first set of experiments.
- Enable shareable post-first-value artifact test.

## 14. Definition of Done
- WFAU measured weekly and trusted.
- D7/D14/W4 reviewed weekly by source and campus.
- 2-3 decision-grade experiments run each week.
- Messaging consistency maintained across web, social, lifecycle, and FAQ.
- Channel scale decisions made only after uncertainty bounds are acceptable.
