# DormWay — Fractional Product & GTM Lead

**Scope: March – August 2026, part-time/fractional**

---

## What We're Building

DormWay is an academic clarity tool for college students. We have a working product, real users, and a concentrated bet: win an incoming freshman cohort (Class of 2030) at the University of Michigan by Fall 2026 move-in.

Two clocks are running:
- **Fundraising**: seed lead by mid-May, round by end of June
- **Freshman conversion**: product ready before August move-in

---

- **Riley** — product vision, brand, founder narrative. Riley *is* the customer — he built DormWay from lived student pain. Owns the story, the voice, and the freshman positioning.
- **Ethan** — technical platform, integrations, reliability, analytics infrastructure. Fractional.
- **Jennifer** — the piece we're missing: a senior product/GTM operator who's built consumer digital products before and can turn vision into phased execution against a hard deadline.

## The Role

**Fractional Product & GTM Lead.** Three people, three lanes:

| Lane | Owner |
|---|---|
| **Product + voice** — Riley and Jennifer shape product together. Riley brings the student lens and brand voice. Jennifer brings structure and experience. | Riley + Jennifer |
| **GTM execution + people** — plans, prioritization, funnel, campaigns, fundraising materials, and managing all external contributors | Jennifer |
| **Platform + scale** — backend, integrations, analytics infra, reliability | Ethan |

### What you'd own

**Product partnership with Riley** — Work with Riley to shape the product roadmap, onboarding flows, and student experience. He brings the conviction and the user empathy. You bring the frameworks and the pattern recognition.

**Execution planning** — Turn product direction into scoped, phased work. Set priorities. Make keep/kill calls. Own the operating cadence.

**Onboarding & activation** — Audit the first-run student experience. Identify and fix trust/activation blockers. Track activation and retention by source and campus.

**GTM** — Own the Class of 2030 freshman wedge campaign. Structure source attribution. Design the landing, content, and conversion path. Produce founder-led content (Riley's the voice, you're the strategist).

**People** — We have contract marketing help, copywriters, and content creators. They need clear deliverables, deadlines, and accountability. You'd manage those relationships and make sure external contributors are producing work that actually moves the plan forward.

**Fundraising support** — Draft the deck and investor narrative from real product proof. Build the demo path and data room.

### What you wouldn't own

- Technical architecture and engineering (Ethan)
- Brand voice origination and founder narrative (Riley)
