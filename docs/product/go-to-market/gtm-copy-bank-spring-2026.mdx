# DormWay Copy Bank (Spring 2026)

Date: 2026-02-22
Voice target: student-first, concrete, trust-forward

## 0. Guardrails (Do Not Drift)

Keep:
- "Free for students" (core promise)
- "No credit card"
- "We use AI for dates, not essays"
- "Read-only access. Revoke anytime."

Avoid until verified or strategically locked:
- "Free forever"
- "No premium tiers" / "No paywalls"
- "Upload anything"
- "Grades pull in automatically"

Preferred replacements:
- "Upload a syllabus"
- "Manual sync" / "Refresh when you want" (for grade surfaces)
- "We build your timeline"

## 7. Objection Handling Snippets

"Is this just another AI app?"
- We use AI for date extraction, not homework writing.
- DormWay is about deadline clarity and planning.

"Is it free?"
- Yes, free for students.
- No credit card required.

"Can this change my Canvas data?"
- No. Read-only access.
- You can revoke access anytime.
- DormWay cannot submit or edit assignments.

"Are grades automatic?"
- Grade tools are manual sync/refresh today.
- We only claim automatic where it is fully reliable.

## 9. Copy QA Checklist
- Is the message outcome-first?
- Is there one clear next action?
- Does it avoid AI hype language?
- Is monetization language aligned with "Free for students (core)"?
- Is there a trust cue near the CTA (read-only/revoke/data)?
- Are grade claims aligned to manual sync wording where needed?
