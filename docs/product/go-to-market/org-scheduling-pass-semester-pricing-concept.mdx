# Org Scheduling Pass - Semester Pricing Concept

## Snapshot

Offer student organizations a low-friction paid plan at **$10 per org per semester** for "When2meet-level" scheduling with recurring event support, availability matching, and reminder automation.

Strategic intent:
- Keep DormWay free for individual students.
- Monetize at the organization layer (club treasury / officer spend).
- Use scheduling utility as a fast, repeated workflow that creates retention.

This concept is aligned with current public positioning in [FAQ (Sept Update)](/docs/product/go-to-market/faq-sept-update) that DormWay does not charge students for core access.

## Problem

Student org officers repeatedly face the same pain:
- Hard to find common free time for 5-40 people.
- Group chats and ad hoc polls are slow and noisy.
- Recurring events are manually re-scheduled each week.
- Students miss events because reminders and follow-ups are fragmented.

Scheduling is frequent enough to monetize, but price sensitivity is high.

## Product Thesis

If DormWay provides the fastest way for officers to find overlap and lock meetings, orgs will pay a small term fee from existing budgets to avoid weekly coordination overhead.

The offer should feel:
- Obvious (saves time immediately).
- Cheap (below petty cash threshold).
- Low risk (starts usable before payment).

## Pricing Model Options

### Option A: Flat Org Pass (recommended)
- Price: $10 per org per semester.
- Included: unlimited polls, recurring polls, best-time recommendations, reminder automation.
- Reason: simplest message and lowest purchase friction.

### Option B: Officer-seat pricing
- Price: $3-5 per officer per month.
- Included: member participation stays free.
- Reason not preferred now: more billing complexity and perceived student subscription risk.

### Option C: Campus bundle
- Price: central campus buyer funds many org licenses.
- Reason not preferred now: better long-term, slower initial sales cycle.

## Recommended Packaging (v1)

### Free
- Create/join basic availability polls.
- Manual slot selection.
- Limited recurring support.

### Org Scheduling Pass ($10/semester)
- Unlimited availability polls.
- Recurring meeting templates (weekly/biweekly/monthly).
- "Best 3 times" ranked by attendance likelihood.
- Officer controls (role-based ownership for events/polls).
- Reminder automation (poll close + event reminder).
- Basic CSV export for attendance/planning handoff.

### Deferred for v2
- SMS credits/add-on automation.
- Org branding/custom links.
- Campus-level reporting and multi-org bundle admin.

## Scope Definition: "Full When2meet Constant Functionality"

Functional baseline for v1:
- Fast overlap matrix and visual free-time selection.
- Recurring event scheduling with one-click reuse.
- Continuous availability refresh from user schedule/time blocks.
- Conflict-aware recommendations, not just raw overlap counts.
- Audit-safe privacy defaults (show aggregate free/busy only).

Non-goals for v1:
- Full institutional calendar integrations for every campus system.
- AI-generated agenda/planning features.
- Advanced attendance analytics beyond core exports.

## Billing and Term Logic

Billing assumptions for v1:
- Processor: Stripe Checkout (card + wallet support).
- Billing unit: org, term-based.
- Default term duration: 6 months if campus term metadata is unavailable.
- Renewal reminder: 14 days before term end.
- Auto-renew: enabled by default, cancel anytime.

Future optimization:
- Derive term windows from campus context where available.

## Go-to-Market Plan

### ICP
- Student organizations with recurring meetings/events:
  - Greek life leadership
  - Cultural clubs
  - Project teams
  - Pre-professional organizations

### Core Channels
- Campus ambassadors and officer referrals.
- Org fair QR flow to "Schedule first meeting in 5 minutes."
- In-product upgrade prompt when free org hits recurring scheduling need.
- Officer handoff kit at semester transitions.

### Activation Funnel
1. Create org
2. Invite 3+ officers
3. Run first availability poll
4. Finalize first event
5. Set recurring template

Activation target: org reaches step 4 within 48 hours.

## Metrics

North star:
- **Paid org term retention** (org renews next semester).

Leading indicators:
- Time-to-first-scheduled-event.
- Poll completion rate.
- Median participants per poll.
- Recommended-slot acceptance rate.
- Support tickets per 100 paid orgs.

Revenue indicators:
- Paid org count by campus.
- Effective term revenue.
- CAC payback by channel.

- Org model + role controls.

- 3-5 campuses.

- Renewal automation.

## Financial Reality Check

Illustrative gross revenue:
- 100 paid orgs = $1,000 per term.
- 500 paid orgs = $5,000 per term.
- 1,500 paid orgs = $15,000 per term.

At $10 ARPU, viability depends on:
- highly automated onboarding,
- low-touch support,
- strong self-serve growth loops.

## Risks and Mitigations

### Risk: ARPU too low for manual support
- Mitigation: product-led onboarding, templates, docs-first support.

### Risk: privacy concerns around schedule visibility
- Mitigation: aggregate availability only; explicit consent and role controls.

### Risk: low willingness to pay
- Mitigation: free-to-value path, first successful event before paywall, term-start promotions.

### Risk: semester churn due to officer turnover
- Mitigation: officer handoff workflow + renewal reminders tied to role transfer.

## Experiments to Run Before Full Commit

1. Price elasticity: `$10` vs `$15` test on checkout conversion + retention.
2. Feature gate test: recurring templates as primary paid trigger vs reminder automation.
3. Payment timing test: pay-before-first-event vs pay-after-first-successful-event.
4. Campus channel test: ambassador-led vs organic officer referrals.

## Open Questions

- Should v1 include SMS reminders or keep notifications in-app/email only?
- Should hidden calendars block auto-scheduling by default?
- Should org plans be per-semester only, or offer annual discount from day one?
- Do we permit one free officer transfer per term to reduce renewal friction?
