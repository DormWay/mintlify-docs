# DormWay GTM Evaluation (Spring 2026)

## Purpose
This document is the cleaned, decision-grade GTM evaluation for Spring 2026.
It supersedes earlier draft language that used artifact citation tokens and optimistic lift ranges without uncertainty bounds.

## Executive Summary
The strategic direction remains correct:
- Keep the deadline-certainty wedge ("Never wonder what's due").
- Run a campus-density strategy.
- Prioritize fast first value.

Operational updates now locked:
1. Campus distribution + in-product sharing loop is the primary growth engine.
2. Start with 5-8 campuses; expand only after repeatability proof.
3. Treat landing upload instrumentation as a scale gate.
4. Replace lift promises with scenario-based planning + confidence intervals.
5. Lock one monetization narrative and remove contradictory copy.

## Best Path Forward
Use a campus-first operating model:
1. Student sees pain-led message.
2. Student gets timeline value quickly.
3. Student shares output with a friend/classmate.
4. Campus density compounds.
5. Lifecycle nudges lock weekly habit.

Channel hierarchy:
- Primary: campus distribution + product sharing loop.
- Secondary: short-form content as feeder.
- Support: lifecycle retention (Sunday preview + due-soon).

## Top Actions (Next 14 Days)
1. Lock and propagate one public monetization narrative across web, FAQ, and scripts.
2. Remove automatic-grade language and align grade messaging to manual sync/refresh wording.
3. Implement and verify landing upload start/completion instrumentation.
4. Establish baseline activation/retention by source and campus with 95% confidence intervals.
5. Launch first-value-before-signup and share-loop experiments with clear keep/kill thresholds.

## Expected Outcomes (Scenarios, Not Promises)

### Scenario A: Measurement-Ready (2-3 weeks)
Definition:
- Landing funnel events are reliable.
- Cohort reporting works by source and campus.
- D7/D14/W4 reporting exists.

Outcome:
- Channel decisions become trustworthy.

### Scenario B: Repeatable Campus Loop (4-8 weeks)
Definition:
- At least 2 campuses show stable WFAU and non-degrading D14/W4.
- Referral/share contributes measurable first actions.

Outcome:
- Expand campus count while preserving activation quality.

### Scenario C: Scale-Ready (8-12 weeks)
Definition:
- 3-4 additional campuses replicate the same activation/retention pattern.

Outcome:
- Controlled expansion toward broader campus coverage.

## Critical Findings

### Highest Severity: Monetization Trust Contradiction
Problem:
- Public "free forever/no premium tiers" language conflicts with internal monetization modeling and event taxonomy.

Risk:
- Credibility erosion and referral drag if monetization story differs across surfaces.

Decision:
- Standardize to: "Free for students (core)."
- Keep paid tests in org/admin and institutional paths.
- Use transparent language for any future optional student-paid features.

### High Severity: Overclaim on Grade Automation
Problem:
- Student-facing messaging has included automatic-grade language while current grade experience uses manual sync/refresh.

Risk:
- Onboarding trust break and activation drop.

Decision:
- Replace automatic-grade wording with expectation-safe manual sync wording everywhere.

### High Severity: Non-Decision-Grade Instrumentation
Problem:
- Landing upload tracking has been an attribution blind spot.

Risk:
- False channel winners and wasted execution cycles.

Decision:
- Gate scaled experiments on verified landing upload attribution.

### Medium Severity: Channel Hierarchy Drift
Problem:
- Short-form can pull focus toward vanity metrics if treated as primary engine.

Decision:
- Evaluate short-form on source-tagged first-action and retention quality only.

### Medium Severity: D7-Only Retention Lens
Problem:
- D7 alone does not capture durability across student weekly and monthly patterns.

Decision:
- Track D7, D14, and W4 together for every major GTM readout.

## Messaging and Copy Decisions
Ship immediately:
- Remove: "Free forever", "No premium tiers", "Upload anything", "Grades pull in automatically".
- Keep: "Free for students", "No credit card", "We use AI for dates, not essays".
- Add near CTA: "Read-only access. Revoke anytime. We can't submit assignments."
- Keep AI language as boundary/trust cue, never headline.

Approved monetization line:
- "Free for students (core). We make money from org/admin tools and institutional pilots. If we ever add optional paid student features, we'll be transparent and keep the free core."

## Experiment Backlog (Decision-Grade)
1. Copy correction pass: remove grade overclaim.
- Success: activation up or stable, D14 not worse.

2. First value before signup.
- Success: higher signup-to-first-action conversion, lower time-to-first-value.

3. Share artifact loop (week-at-a-glance + referral link).
- Success: referral-start to referral-first-action conversion improves.

4. Campus officer wedge.
- Success: org-linked cohorts outperform baseline retention.

5. Sunday preview lifecycle + one due-soon nudge.
- Success: Monday/Tuesday active lift and D14 improvement vs control.

## KPI and Measurement Framework
Primary:
- WFAU rate
- Activation rate
- D7, D14, W4 retention

Secondary:
- Time-to-first-value
- First-action mix
- Campus density
- Ambassador/referral quality

Readout standards:
- Report point estimate + 95% CI for rate metrics.
- Require minimum practical effect + uncertainty clearance before scaling.
- Review by source and by campus, not blended only.
