# Product Capabilities

DormWay is broad, but the product makes the most sense when grouped by student jobs instead of a raw feature dump. The core theme across every capability is the same: **DormWay already knows the campus and college town you're walking into, then merges what school tells you on top — and keeps it current.** That world model plus the multi-source merge is the structural difference from a generic planner or a study-materials tool.

<Note>
  Naming: the collaboration surface is **Mix** (renamed from "Crews" around 2026-04-25). Use "Mix" in product-facing prose. Code identifiers still read `crews` / `crew_*` (routes, the `feature.crews_enabled` flag), which is why engineering references below use both.
</Note>

---

## 1. The Campus + College-Town World Model

This is DormWay's deepest differentiator. Before a student uploads anything, DormWay carries a model of their **campus and their college town**, organized as a hierarchy: Country → State → City → Campus → Building → Course → Student.

The world model can include:

- The school's **real academic calendar** — add/drop, finals, per-campus registrar deadlines, term boundaries
- **Campus and city context** — weather, dining, campus + city events, safety / news alerts, transportation and parking, housing
- The campus **student graph** that ties courses, buildings, and students to the right place and term

<Note>
  **Coverage vs depth — important distinction.** The *structural* world model spans effectively every US campus and town (grounded in the database on 2026-05-30: 6,456 campus configs, 2,884 college-town city configs, 2,891 city + 28,983 building contexts). The *deep LLM enrichment* — events, news, safety, and logistics — is **live on pilot campuses today and rolling out target-campus-first**, not yet deep on every campus. Claim the capability; don't claim deep knowledge of all campuses.
</Note>

Why it matters: a study-materials tool only knows the document you paste, and a planner only knows the tasks from one LMS. Neither carries any campus or world context. DormWay does — and then merges the student's own sources on top of it.

## 2. Multi-Source Merge

DormWay's second structural capability is turning fragmented academic obligations into one coherent, **sourced** timeline and task list.

- **Cross-source merge and deduplication** of Canvas, syllabus extraction, schedule imports, calendar sources, and manual inputs — so the same event does not keep appearing as separate realities
- **Provenance** — anything shown as certain stays source-backed; source data stays canonical while student edits layer on top
- **Schedule import** from PDFs, screenshots, and calendar sources, normalized into time-aware blocks linked to the right course, term, and campus
- **Explicit future-term setup** that uses the selected term's campus calendar dates instead of the currently active term's
- **Manual time blocks** for study, meetings, office hours, or personal events; **recurring schedule support** where the source data provides it

This is the difference from a generic planner: the product is not waiting for the student to type every assignment into existence.

## 3. Awareness and Daily Triage

The highest-value moment in DormWay is fast awareness. The product should help a student answer "am I okay?" before it asks them to do anything more ambitious.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Today" icon="sun">
    The default check-in surface. It surfaces what needs attention now, not just a pretty timeline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Day plan" icon="clock">
    A fresh plan can be generated each morning from the student's confirmed schedule, deadlines, and context.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Heads Up" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Urgency-first callouts for things that could bite the student if ignored.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Finite by design" icon="check">
    Calm, bounded awareness — **not a feed** that keeps students scrolling.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Key product rules for this layer:

- Priority beats pure chronology
- The screen should never create false confidence
- The default should work with no customization
- Anything that looks certain should be source-backed

## 4. Task Consolidation

- **Multi-source task list** combining Canvas, syllabus extraction, and manual inputs
- **Urgency grouping** so overdue and due-soon work rises above the backlog
- **Planning actions** that turn work into scheduled time instead of leaving it abstract
- **Capacity and workload framing** so a student can see when the week is genuinely overloaded

## 5. Syllabus and Course Intelligence

Uploading a syllabus is one of DormWay's strongest activation moments because it unlocks information students usually only have buried inside a PDF.

- **Deadline extraction** for exams, assignments, quizzes, and recurring course events
- **Policy extraction** for grading, attendance, late work, office hours, and course rules
- **Date normalization** across term boundaries and recurring patterns
- **Course intelligence** such as realistic weekly hours, hidden workload, red flags, and opportunities
- **Term-level insight** like peak weeks, workload clusters, and collision detection across courses
- **Conflict computation** that distinguishes low-stakes pileups from genuinely dangerous clusters

The goal is not just "read the syllabus" — it is "turn syllabus information into something operationally useful all semester."

## 6. Sync, Realtime, and Smart Delivery

DormWay is designed so the student does not have to babysit freshness.

- **Canvas connection** for courses, assignments, announcements, and grade changes
- **Calendar and schedule syncs** evaluated on recurring cadences
- **Read-replica routing for eligible read-heavy API paths** so public, mobile, and dashboard reads can avoid unnecessary primary load
- **Queue-bound schedule processing direction** so background reconciliation does not starve onboarding or explicit imports
- **Ably-powered realtime delivery** for important changes to student state
- **Smart notification timing** that treats student attention as finite instead of blasting everything immediately
- **Context-aware updates** when a class changes, a deadline moves, or the schedule meaningfully shifts

This layer mostly shows up as "DormWay stayed right without me touching it." That is the point.

## 7. Ace and Document Intelligence

Ace is the conversational layer on top of the student's own academic context. It should feel like the student is talking to their semester, not a generic chatbot.

- **Course-aware chat** using the student's courses, deadlines, and documents
- **Document question-answering** grounded in uploaded materials and syllabus context — Ace answers **across your class materials**
- **Task-aware actions** where read operations are instant and mutations require confirmation
- **A tighter trust boundary** than generic AI tools

<Note>
  **AI boundary (brand canon):** DormWay uses **AI for your deadlines, not your essays**. Ace organizes and answers across your school — it does not do your work or fabricate certainty. This is a deliberate trust position, not a gap.
</Note>

## 8. Library, Notes, and Study Materials

- **Library surfaces** for recent lectures, notes, recordings, and course materials
- **Document-aware workflows** that let Ace and other tools answer against those materials
- **Richer note and lecture experiences on larger Apple surfaces**, including PencilKit-based workflows where available

The long-term value is continuity: deadlines, schedule, notes, and materials should feel like parts of the same system.

## 9. Collaboration Through Mix

Mix is DormWay's multiplayer layer — academic, student-aware shared spaces anchored to a real thing (a room, a class, a project), private to who you invite. It is **not a social network and not a feed**: every Mix leads with the job, never "connect with friends."

**Six Mix types** (code: `crews`):

| Type | What it coordinates |
|------|---------------------|
| **Coordination** | Logistics like move-in, shared rides, shared spaces |
| **Study** | A study group with shared class deadlines |
| **Project** | A project team with claimable tasks and shared files |
| **Event** | Planning an event — polls, scheduling, chat |
| **Dorm** | A building: amenities, maintenance, quiet hours, floor events |
| **Room** | Roommates: shared checklist, chat, before move-in |

Each Mix can include claimable tasks, **Find Times**, chat, files, a notebook, events, and deadline reminders with course-deadline sync.

- **Find Times** (live) — collect availability, suggest meeting times, confirm; shareable via a public code so anyone can tap their free times without an account or install
- **Invites are link/code-based, not email** — a creator generates an invite code (`/join/[code]`); a joiner enters a display name (email optional) and joins as a **guest with no account**, and can convert to a full account later
- **Deadline-aware group spaces** when the Mix is tied to academic work
- **Shared files plus document Q&A** for the group's own materials

<Note>
  **Current production posture (2026-05-30):** Mix is built and live (six types, Find Times, chat, files, notebook, tasks, deadline sync; shipped in iOS v1.0; `feature.crews_enabled` defaults ON). **Joining** a Mix is open; **creating** one is currently allowlist-gated (`plg.crews.creator_allowlist`).

  **Planned (decided 2026-05-30, DORM-1518, pending build + Riley sign-off):** removing the creator allowlist so any student can start a Mix, an email quick-start flow for room setup, a standalone `/mix` marketing page, and **Mix Pro** — a "coming" tier adding cross-term rosters, archiving, and org-scale features. None of these are live yet.
</Note>

## 10. Personalization and Platform-Specific Features

DormWay supports adaptation without making customization a prerequisite for value.

### Web

- Persistent layout on Today
- Drag-and-drop reordering, widget resizing
- Hide and restore behavior, add-widget flows
- Shape-adaptive widgets that reinterpret themselves based on available space

### iPhone and iPad

- Native SwiftUI client; iOS v1.0 tabs are **Today · Tasks · Schedule · Courses · Mix** (Ace lives in Today)
- Home screen widgets via WidgetKit
- Live Activities and alarm-based time-awareness on supported devices
- Deeper note-taking and study workflows on iPad-class surfaces

The principle is straightforward: **great defaults first, customization available second**.

## 11. Pre-Semester and the High-School Lifecycle

DormWay starts working **before** a student is enrolled — a lifecycle neither study-materials tools nor planners have.

- A high-school senior can enroll as their incoming cohort (e.g., Class of 2030), pick their campus, and DormWay sets up their academic world — calendar, deadlines, and the campus world model — before day one
- **Pre-semester signup** runs for incoming students before a fall schedule or Canvas account exists, with deadline dispatch tied to the campus calendar

<Note>
  This is built and on-thesis (the Class of 2030 wedge) but **nascent** — frame it as "we start in high school," not as a claim about large existing high-school usage.
</Note>

## 12. Public Entry, Activation, and Growth Programs

DormWay has real product surface area before the authenticated workspace, and the public layer is real utility rather than a demo.

- **Grade calculator** as a no-auth value surface
- **Campus and course SEO pages** that expose real school and syllabus intelligence
- **Guest / shareable patterns** (including Find Times public codes and guest Mix join) that show value before full setup
- **DormWay Labs** — the paid student task pool for syllabus uploads, QA, research, and bug reports; part product operations, part growth engine, part quality loop. See [DormWay Labs](/docs/overview/dormway-labs).
