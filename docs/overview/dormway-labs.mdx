# DormWay Labs

DormWay Labs is a community-driven task pool where students earn money by helping improve the platform. Students apply to join, browse available tasks, and claim them first-come-first-served. All work is asynchronous, done in-product, and paid weekly.

---

## How It Works

Labs uses a **self-service First-Come-First-Served (FCFS) model**:

1. **Apply** — Submit a quick application from `/dashboard/programs`
2. **Get Approved** — Applications are reviewed within 24–48 hours
3. **Browse the Task Pool** — See available tasks with payouts and deadlines
4. **Claim a Task** — Grab it first-come-first-served; it disappears from the pool immediately (real-time update)
5. **Complete and Submit** — Submit your work (URL or notes)
6. **Get Paid** — Approved work is paid weekly, typically on Fridays

---

## Task Types and Rewards

| Task Type | Description | Typical Payout |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Syllabus Upload** | Upload course syllabi from your campus | $2–5 per syllabus |
| **Bug Report** | Find and document bugs in the app | $5–20 based on severity |
| **QA Testing** | Test specific features and report issues | $5–15 per session |
| **Research** | User interviews, surveys, feedback | $10–25 per task |
| **Other** | Custom tasks as needed | Varies |

Active Labs members working a few hours per week typically earn $50–200/month.

## Submitting Work

1. Click **Submit** on your task card
2. Provide one or both:
   - **URL** — Link to your work (Google Drive, GitHub, etc.)
   - **Notes** — Description of what you did
3. Click **Submit for Review**
4. Wait for admin review (usually 24–48 hours)

**Tips for approval:**
- Follow task instructions exactly
- For syllabi: ensure the PDF is readable and contains a grading breakdown and course schedule
- For bugs: include steps to reproduce, expected vs. actual behavior, and a screenshot or screen recording
- Submit early — do not wait until the deadline

---

## Quality Guidelines

### Syllabus Uploads
- PDF is readable (not a blurry scan)
- Contains grading breakdown
- Contains course schedule
- Matches the course it is uploaded for
- Not a duplicate of an existing syllabus

### Bug Reports
- Clear, descriptive title
- Steps to reproduce
- Expected vs. actual behavior
- Screenshot or screen recording
- Platform info (iOS / web, browser, version)

---

## Deadlines and Task Management

- **Deadlines matter** — Submit before the deadline or the task returns to the pool automatically
- **Unclaim if needed** — Can't finish? Unclaim the task so another member can claim it
- **Don't over-claim** — Only claim what you can actually complete
- **Reliability** — Frequent unclaiming or missed deadlines may affect future eligibility

---

## Payment Terms

- **Contractor status** — All Labs rewards are paid as 1099 independent contractor income
- **Tax responsibility** — Participants are responsible for applicable taxes on earnings
- **Weekly payouts** — Approved tasks are batched and paid weekly (typically Fridays)
- **Payout method** — Direct deposit or PayPal (configured during onboarding)
- **Confidentiality** — Participants agree to confidentiality regarding non-public product information
- **Terms subject to change** — DormWay reserves the right to modify reward amounts and program terms

## Application Process

1. Sign up for DormWay (if not already a user)
2. Navigate to **Programs** (`/dashboard/programs`)
3. Click **Apply** on the Labs card
4. Fill out the application:
   - Why do you want to join Labs?
   - Your social media reach (helps understand your network)
   - Your campus
5. Wait for approval (24–48 hours)
6. Start claiming tasks

**Approval criteria:** Clear motivation to help improve DormWay, enrollment at an active or target campus, no red flags in application. Applications are generously approved — the task pool model is self-regulating, so poor performers naturally claim fewer tasks.

## Labs and Other Programs

DormWay also offers Ambassador and Creators programs. They are independent but complementary:

| Labs | Ambassador | Creators |
|------|------------|----------|
| Task-based work | Referral-based earnings | Content + referral earnings |
| In-product tasks | Campus outreach | Social/video-driven campus growth |
| Earn per task completed | Earn per successful referral | Earn via creator payout model |

Students can participate in multiple programs simultaneously for multiple earning streams.

## API Reference

For developers integrating with the Labs system:

| Endpoint | Method | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| `/v2/students/me/programs` | GET | Check Labs, Ambassador, and Creators status |
| `/v2/students/me/programs/labs/apply` | POST | Apply to Labs |
| `/admin/labs/pool` | GET | Get available tasks |
| `/admin/labs/tasks/:id/claim` | POST | Claim a task |
| `/admin/labs/tasks/:id/unclaim` | POST | Unclaim a task |
| `/admin/labs/tasks/:id` | PATCH | Submit completed task |
