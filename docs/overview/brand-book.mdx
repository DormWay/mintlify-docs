# Brand Book

This page captures DormWay's brand fundamentals: voice, positioning, naming conventions, and visual identity basics. A comprehensive brand style guide is maintained separately by the design and marketing teams.

<Note>
  This page reflects brand guidelines as of March 2026. For the latest creative assets (logos, color tokens, typography files), contact the design team.
</Note>

---

## Brand Positioning

**What DormWay is:**
A student OS — an AI-powered platform that helps college students manage their academic lives in one place. Not just a planner, not just a to-do list, not just an AI chatbot: a unified operating system for college.

**Who it's for:**
College students at US universities and colleges — from large state schools to small liberal arts colleges. Students who are juggling Canvas assignments, group projects, unpredictable schedules, and the chaos of dorm and campus life.

**Core value proposition:**
DormWay gives students smart context about what matters right now. It absorbs the complexity of academic coordination — syncing Canvas, parsing syllabi, tracking deadlines, scheduling study time — so students can focus on learning.

**Founder story:**
DormWay was built by Riley Crimmins, a University of Michigan student, out of lived need. The insight: students don't just need a calendar — they need a system that understands their campus, their weather, their walking time to class, and their actual workload. That specific, grounded need is what DormWay is built around.

## Brand Voice

DormWay speaks like a knowledgeable peer — not a corporate product, not a quirky startup. The voice is:

**Clear over clever.** DormWay explains things simply. Avoid jargon. When technical terms are necessary, define them.

**Confident but not arrogant.** DormWay makes definitive statements about what it does. It does not over-hedge or use filler phrases like "designed to help you possibly maybe..."

**Student-first.** Copy speaks to the student's actual problems: deadline panic, disorganization, feeling behind. Not abstract productivity goals.

**Direct.** Short sentences. No fluff. Get to the point.

**Examples of voice:**

| Instead of... | Write... |
|--------------|---------|
| "DormWay is designed to potentially help students manage their academic workload more effectively." | "DormWay keeps your classes, assignments, and schedule in one place." |
| "We leverage cutting-edge AI to provide intelligent insights." | "DormWay reads your syllabi and tells you when your semester gets rough." |
| "Our platform offers a comprehensive suite of tools." | "Schedule. Tasks. Ace AI. Canvas sync. All in one app." |

## Naming Conventions

### The Product

- **DormWay** — Always capitalized as two words run together: capital D, capital W, no space, no hyphen.
  - Correct: DormWay
  - Wrong: Dormway, dormway, Dorm-Way, Dorm Way

- **LockedIn** — The internal name for the web application. Not used in public-facing copy. The web app is "DormWay" or "the DormWay web app" externally.

### Features and Products

| Name | Usage |
|------|-------|
| **Ace** | DormWay's AI assistant. "Ace" alone is sufficient; "Ace AI" or "Ace assistant" for clarity in context. |
| **Crews** | The group collaboration feature. "DormWay Crews" when the context requires the full product name. |
| **Labs** | The student task-pool program. "DormWay Labs" formally; "Labs" in context. |
| **Canvas** | Always "Canvas" (not "Canvas LMS" unless needed for clarity). |
| **Term Insights** | Course-level semester analysis tab. Capitalized as a proper feature name. |
| **Syllabus Intelligence** | The AI syllabus analysis feature. Both words capitalized. |
| **Heads Up** | The AI priority alert widget. Both words capitalized. |
| **Find Times** | The Crews scheduling feature. Both words capitalized. |
| **Deadline Sync** | The Crews Canvas integration feature. Both words capitalized. |

### Platforms

- **DormWay** or **DormWay web** — the web application at app.dormway.com
- **DormWay iOS** — the iPhone and iPad app
- Avoid "LockedIn" in public copy

### AI References

- The AI assistant is **Ace** — a proper name, not an acronym
- Avoid "our AI" or "powered by GPT" — say "Ace" or "DormWay's AI"
- "AI crew" or "AI-powered" is acceptable in technical context

## Visual Identity

### Logo

The DormWay wordmark uses the "DormWay" logotype with the capital D and capital W. Logo files are provided in SVG and PNG formats for light and dark backgrounds.

<Warning>
  Do not recreate the logo from scratch. Always use approved asset files from the design team.
</Warning>

### Colors

DormWay supports six user-selectable themes (Dark, Light, Glass, Paper, Calm, System). Internal brand colors for marketing materials:

- **Primary** — The DormWay brand accent; used in CTAs and primary actions
- **Surface colors** — `--ds-surface-1`, `--ds-surface-2`, `--ds-surface-3` CSS variables (defined in the design system)
- **Border** — `--ds-border-1` CSS variable

For marketing and press materials, use the approved Figma color tokens. Do not hardcode hex values from the web app — the design system uses semantic tokens.

### Typography

The DormWay web app uses a system font stack optimized for readability on web and iOS. Marketing materials use the brand typeface defined in Figma. Contact the design team for font licenses and usage guidelines.

### Themes

DormWay users can choose from six visual themes. When showing screenshots or demos:
- Use **Dark** theme as the default (it is the most visually distinctive)
- Use **Glass** for visually rich presentations
- Match the theme to the audience (dark for developer/technical audiences, glass for consumer/marketing)

## Product Copy Guidelines

### App Store / Marketing Copy

- Lead with the student problem, not the feature
- Use present tense: "DormWay reads your syllabus" not "DormWay will read your syllabus"
- Specific beats vague: "6 customizable widgets" beats "powerful dashboard"
- Keep headlines under 10 words

### In-App Microcopy

- Use action verbs: "Upload syllabus", "Connect Canvas", "Plan this task"
- Empty states should motivate action: "No tasks yet — sync Canvas to get started"
- Error messages should tell the user what to do, not just what went wrong

### Technical Documentation

- This docs site (docs.dormway.app) targets developers, engineers, and internal teams
- Use precise technical language — "Temporal workflow", "Ably pub/sub", "RSC boundary"
- Link to code rather than repeating implementation details

## DormWay vs. Competitors

DormWay's differentiation in brief:

| Competitor | What They Do | What DormWay Adds |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Canvas | LMS (courses/assignments) | Schedule, AI insights, day plans, cross-course analysis |
| Google Calendar | Calendar | Academic awareness, Canvas sync, AI prioritization |
| Notion | Notes/workspace | Academic context, automatic Canvas sync, mobile-native |
| Turbo AI | AI study tools | Full schedule + task management, iOS, Crews, campus data |
| GroupMe/Discord | Group messaging | Academic context, smart scheduling, deadline sync |

The positioning statement: **DormWay does what none of these do alone — and connects them all.**
