# Brand Book

This page documents the current **verbal** brand system for DormWay. It is the source to use when writing product pages, landing pages, app copy, investor materials, support content, and social posts.

<Note>
  Visual assets, Figma components, and design-system tokens are maintained outside Mintlify. This page is intentionally focused on positioning, language, and framing rules.
</Note>

---

## Positioning Essentials

| Topic | Current rule |
|-------|--------------|
| **Public promise** | **Never wonder what's due.** |
| **Internal frame** | **Crisis prevention infrastructure for college students.** |
| **AI boundary** | **We use AI for dates, not essays.** |
| **Monetization line** | **Free for students** at the core level |

### What DormWay is

DormWay is a zero-maintenance academic layer that updates itself from real school data and helps students understand what matters now. It is not just a planner, not just a to-do list, and not just a chatbot.

### What DormWay is not

- Not a corporate EdTech platform
- Not an AI company that happens to touch education
- Not a generic productivity tool for overachievers
- Not a replacement for Canvas or university systems
- Not a homework or essay automation product

The product should always sound like it was built by a student who knows the problem firsthand, because that is true.

## Messaging Hierarchy

Every high-quality DormWay page follows the same order:

1. **Problem**: name the student pain in concrete language
2. **Promise**: show the better state in one sentence
3. **Proof**: give specific, trust-building facts
4. **Mechanism**: explain how DormWay gets there without sounding like infrastructure docs

### Strong problem language

- "Canvas has half your deadlines. Your syllabus has the rest. Your calendar has neither."
- "You're not bad at school. You're bad at tracking six different places where school happens."
- "Ever realize at 11pm something's due tomorrow?"
- "The Sunday-night scramble."

### Strong mechanism language

- "Upload a syllabus. Connect Canvas. See everything."
- "5 minutes now, zero effort for the rest of the semester."
- "Fresh day plan every morning. Hourly syncs. Realtime updates."
- "One screen. Every deadline. Zero effort."

## Voice System

The formula is simple:

**Friendly first, credible second, never corporate.**

### Rules

- **Lead with the student's problem, not DormWay's feature list**
- **Use concrete nouns and real scenarios instead of abstract promises**
- **Be honest about limits; do not overclaim**
- **Sound like a helpful, smart student, not a marketing team**
- **Keep sentences clean and direct**
- **Use AI language mostly as a trust boundary, not as the headline**

### Example rewrites

| Instead of this | Write this |
|-----------------|------------|
| "DormWay is designed to potentially help students manage their academic workload more effectively." | "DormWay keeps your classes, assignments, and deadlines in one place." |
| "We leverage cutting-edge AI to provide intelligent insights." | "DormWay reads your syllabi and keeps your deadlines current." |
| "Our comprehensive platform optimizes your semester." | "Know what's due this week without building your own system." |

## Approved Language

| Category | Preferred phrasing |
|----------|--------------------|
| **Value prop** | "Never wonder what's due" / "Every deadline, every class, one screen, zero effort" / "5 min setup for an automated semester" |
| **Student pain** | "Canvas has half your deadlines" / "Sunday-night scramble" / "Ever realize at 11pm something's due?" |
| **Mechanism** | "Upload syllabus. Connect Canvas. See everything." / "Updates itself" / "Fresh day plan every morning" |
| **Trust** | "We use AI for dates, not essays" / "Read-only access" / "Your data stays private" |
| **Ace** | "Chat with your classes" / "Ask Ace about your course materials" / "Ace knows your courses" |

### Words that usually fit the brand

- Today, tomorrow, this week, next seven days
- Upload, connect, see, know, glance, check
- Syllabus, Canvas, calendar, deadline, assignment, semester
- Fresh, merged, synced, automated, clear

## Banned Language

Do not publish copy that makes DormWay sound like corporate software, AI hype, or a fake "study buddy."

| Avoid | Use instead |
|-------|-------------|
| "AI-powered" as the headline | Lead with the student outcome |
| "AI study buddy" / "digital companion" | "Ace" or "chat assistant" |
| "Optimize your semester" | "Know what's due" |
| "Command center" / "autopilot" | "One place for everything" or "Today" |
| "Free forever" / "no premium tiers" | "Free for students" |
| "Digital twin" in student-facing copy | Explain the benefit, not the implementation |

Banned buzzwords include: synergize, optimize, leverage, paradigm, ecosystem, holistic, next-gen, revolutionary, disruptive, game-changer, cutting-edge.

## Naming Conventions

| Name | Rule |
|------|------|
| **DormWay** | Always capital D, capital W, no space, no hyphen |
| **LockedIn** | Internal web-app name only; avoid it in public-facing copy |
| **Ace** | Proper name for the assistant; use "Ace" or "Ace chat" rather than generic AI labels |
| **Crews** | Group coordination product; "DormWay Crews" when context needs the product name |
| **Labs** | Student task-pool program; "DormWay Labs" formally, "Labs" in context |
| **Canvas** | Use "Canvas" unless "Canvas LMS" is needed for clarity |
| **Syllabus Intelligence / Term Insights / Heads Up / Find Times** | Treat as named product features and capitalize accordingly |

## Riley Test

Before publishing anything student-facing, ask:

1. Would Riley send this exact wording to a friend?
2. Does it sound like a real student wrote it?
3. Did we lead with the student's problem instead of our technology?
4. Did we avoid corporate language and AI hype?
5. Are we being transparent about how the product works and what it does not do?

If the answer is no to any of those, rewrite before publishing.
