# For Investors

This page is a technical brief for investors, advisors, and partners who want to understand what makes DormWay architecturally defensible — not just a feature list, but a systems view of the platform's structural advantages.

---

## The Problem at Scale

College students juggle dozens of competing obligations: Canvas assignments across multiple courses, class schedules, group projects, dorm logistics, campus events. These are managed across a fragmented stack of Canvas, Google Calendar, Discord, GroupMe, Notion, When2Meet, and sticky notes.

**The core insight:** Existing tools are context-blind. They do not know when your next class starts, what your syllabus says about the midterm, whether your project team has a deadline conflict, or how far your campus is from your dorm in 20°F weather. DormWay does.

## Technical Differentiation

### 1. The Context System

DormWay's data model is built around a unified **contexts table** — a hierarchical entity store representing cities, campuses, buildings, courses, and students in a single schema. Everything in the platform is anchored to a context.

This means DormWay can reason about multi-dimensional relationships that no general-purpose calendar or task tool can:
- A student's course context knows the course's deadlines, grading weight, and professor
- A student's campus context knows the campus location, timezone, dining hours, and local weather
- A student's term context knows the semester's exam periods, holidays, and registration deadlines

When the StudentWatcher digital twin generates a day plan, it queries the student's context graph — not a flat list of events.

### 2. The StudentWatcher Digital Twin

Every enrolled student has a continuously-running **Temporal workflow** (StudentWatcher) that:
- Monitors their schedule for upcoming classes and deadlines
- Triggers day plan generation at 05:00 campus local time
- Manages context transitions (current class, next event) and publishes them in real time
- Handles Canvas sync signals, syllabus processing completions, and term transitions

This is not a cron job. Temporal's durable workflow execution means StudentWatcher survives server restarts, handles retries transparently, and maintains execution history. At scale, each student is a lightweight, persistent computational unit.

### 3. AI Syllabus Processing

Uploading a syllabus is one of the highest-value onboarding moments. DormWay's **CrewAI multi-agent pipeline** processes each uploaded syllabus through a chain of specialized agents:

- Deadline extractor — identifies every exam, assignment, project, and quiz date
- Policy analyzer — extracts grading breakdown, attendance rules, late work policy
- Insights generator — produces professor-specific advice, peak week identification, conflict analysis
- Semester expander — converts recurring patterns ("weekly readings") into individual dated tasks

The output is a structured JSON document stored in `service_data` and surfaced as **Syllabus Intelligence** — a feature that has no direct equivalent in any competitive product.

### 4. Real-Time Context Architecture

DormWay uses Ably pub/sub throughout the platform. Grade postings, new assignments, schedule changes, and context transitions (e.g., "class started 3 minutes ago") all fire as real-time events — no polling, no stale data.

The StudentWatcher workflow is the event producer. When a student's context changes, it publishes to their Ably channel. Web and iOS clients receive the update immediately. This architecture means the platform is always showing the student what matters *right now*, not what was true at last refresh.

### 5. Crews: Context-Aware Group Coordination

Crews is a group coordination layer that solves the problem no generic tool can: groups that understand academic context.

A Study Crew linked to "CHEM 201" automatically pulls that course's Canvas deadlines into the crew's shared task list. A Project Crew knows when the group project deliverable is due and fires reminders at 24 hours and 1 hour. A Dorm Crew knows the building context.

This is technically distinct from Discord or GroupMe because the Crew is anchored to a DormWay context — it has access to the same entity graph the rest of the platform uses.

Crews also includes AI-powered document Q&A (via Ragie RAG): crew members can upload study guides, project files, or syllabi to a shared library, then ask natural-language questions and receive streaming answers sourced from their own documents.

### 6. Campus Intelligence at Scale

DormWay has processed and indexed data for 6,138+ US colleges and universities — campus locations, local weather integrations, academic calendar awareness, and walking-time calculations. This campus graph powers:
- Time-to-class calculations ("you need to leave in 8 minutes")
- Weather-aware scheduling ("dress for 15°F, it's a 12-minute walk")
- Campus-specific context in Ace AI responses
- 6,138+ individual university landing pages for SEO and campus-specific onboarding

This dataset took significant effort to build and creates a meaningful moat against competitors starting from scratch.

## Infrastructure and Scalability

The platform is built for multi-tenant scale:

- **Railway** hosting with per-service containers — each service scales independently
- **Neon serverless PostgreSQL** — autoscales with query load; branch-per-developer model for safe development
- **Temporal** for workflow orchestration — StudentWatcher digital twins are lightweight and durable; thousands can run concurrently
- **Ably** for realtime — handles connection management and message delivery at scale without infrastructure management
- **Doppler** for secrets — zero hardcoded credentials, environment parity across dev/staging/production

The StudentWatcher model means adding a new student adds one more lightweight Temporal workflow — not a new database partition or cron configuration.

## Technology Bets

DormWay's technology choices reflect deliberate bets:

1. **Temporal over cron** — Durable workflows survive failures, provide execution history, and scale horizontally. The StudentWatcher model gives every student a persistent computational identity.

2. **Neon over managed RDS** — Instant branching for developers, serverless scaling, and branch-per-developer removes infrastructure friction from the development loop.

3. **CrewAI over single-model pipelines** — Multi-agent pipelines allow specialized agents for each extraction task. Syllabus analysis requires different capabilities at each step; a single prompt cannot match a pipeline with separation of concerns.

4. **Ably over WebSockets** — Managed pub/sub at scale without the operational burden of maintaining WebSocket infrastructure. Connection management, presence, and message history are handled by the platform.

5. **Ragie for RAG** — Crew document Q&A needs accurate sourcing and streaming. Ragie provides document indexing, retrieval, and streaming responses without building a vector database pipeline.
