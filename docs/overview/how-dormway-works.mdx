# How DormWay Works

DormWay is built around one low-maintenance loop: **DormWay already knows the campus and college town you're walking into**, you hand it a real source or two, it merges and maintains the semester in the background, and you check **Today** in seconds instead of managing a planner by hand.

It does not replace Canvas, and it is not asking the student to build a second life-management system. The job is to merge what school already knows — on top of a model of your campus — and keep it current.

<Note>
  **AI boundary (brand canon):** DormWay uses **AI for your deadlines, not your essays**. AI is used to extract, normalize, and rank school data. It does **not** write essays, do homework, or pretend to know things it cannot verify.
</Note>

## 0. DormWay Already Knows Your Campus

Before a student uploads anything, DormWay carries a model of their **campus and their college town** — the school's real academic calendar (add/drop, finals, per-campus registrar deadlines), plus weather, dining, events, safety / news alerts, transportation, and housing, organized as a hierarchy (Country → State → City → Campus → Building → Course → Student).

This is the structural difference from a study-materials tool (which only knows the document you paste) or a planner (which only knows the tasks from one LMS): DormWay starts with the world you're walking into, then merges your sources on top.

<Note>
  The *structural* world model spans effectively every US campus and town. The *deep enrichment* (events, news, safety, logistics) is **live on pilot campuses today and rolling out target-campus-first** — not yet deep on every campus.
</Note>

## 1. Value Can Start Before Full Setup

DormWay is no longer only an "after signup" product. Students can encounter it through public or shareable surfaces first, then convert into the authenticated workspace once they have already felt the value.

Current examples:

- the public grade calculator
- campus and course landing pages
- guest / shareable patterns that preview real utility before signup (including a Find Times link or a guest Mix join)

That changes the activation model: DormWay should not make every student start from a blank dashboard.

## 2. Reach First Value Fast Inside the Product

DormWay's canonical first-value actions are simple. Any one of them unlocks first value, because first value means the student can see upcoming deadlines and obligations in one place.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Upload a syllabus" icon="file-arrow-up">
    DormWay extracts deadlines, grading rules, recurring academic events, and term-level risk signals from the PDF.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Import a schedule" icon="calendar-days">
    A PDF, screenshot, or calendar source becomes structured class blocks, course links, and time-aware context.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connect Canvas" icon="link">
    Courses, assignments, announcements, and grade changes start syncing into the same student view.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For incoming students, **pre-semester signup** is also a first action: a high-school senior enrolls as their incoming cohort and picks their campus before a fall schedule or Canvas account exists. (This is built and on-thesis for the Class of 2030 wedge, but nascent — "we start in high school," not a claim about large existing high-school usage.)

Onboarding should make at least one of these actions obvious. DormWay should never force a student through confusing branching before they can see something useful.

## 3. DormWay Builds the Semester Model (the Merge)

Once a student gives DormWay a real source, the platform does the merge work the student should not have to do manually:

- Deadlines are extracted from syllabi and LMS data
- Schedule events are normalized into a single timeline
- Course contexts are linked to the right term, campus, and student
- The same event from Canvas, a syllabus, and a calendar is **deduplicated into one sourced reality**
- Conflicts, peak weeks, and hidden workload get computed in the background
- Source data stays canonical, while student edits layer on top

This is where DormWay stops being "a planner" and becomes a maintained academic layer. The student adds a source; the product keeps it alive. The merge is something neither a single-LMS planner nor a per-document study tool does.

---

## 4. StudentWatcher Keeps It Current

Behind the scenes, each enrolled student is backed by a long-running workflow called **StudentWatcher**. Its job is to keep the student's academic state fresh without making them re-enter data all semester.

- A fresh day plan can be generated at **5:00 AM campus local time**
- Calendar and Canvas syncs are evaluated on a recurring cadence
- Important changes can push through in real time
- Context shifts can trigger plan and notification updates
- The student's state survives restarts, retries, and normal infrastructure churn

Recent engineering work also started moving timer-heavy delivery out of the watcher and into dedicated routers. The student should still feel one coherent system; underneath, the product is getting better at separating state ownership from timed delivery.

Students should feel the result of this system, not its complexity. The ideal is: open the app, glance once, know where you stand.

---

## 5. Today Is the Default Check-In

Today is not an infinite dashboard or a discovery feed. It exists to answer one question quickly:

**"Am I okay, and what do I need to deal with?"**

That shapes the behavior:

- Priority comes before pure chronology
- Urgent obligations are visible without hunting
- The screen should feel finite and calm, **not a feed**
- The default view works immediately, with customization available but never required
- Anything shown as certain should be source-verifiable

On web, Today already supports a real customization layer: persistent layout, drag-and-drop reordering, widget resizing, hide/restore, and add-widget behavior. The main issue today is discoverability of that power, not its absence.

## 6. Students Go Deeper Only When They Need To

Today is the glance surface. The deeper surfaces exist for the moments when a student needs more detail or wants to act.

| Surface | What students use it for |
|---------|--------------------------|
| **Tasks** | Review assignments and obligations from multiple sources in one place |
| **Schedule** | See classes, time blocks, and calendar events in a normalized timeline |
| **Courses / Syllabus Intelligence** | Understand one course deeply: workload, policies, key dates, red flags |
| **Library / Notes** | Revisit documents, notes, and course materials |
| **Ace** | Ask questions across your own courses, schedule, and class materials |
| **Mix** | Coordinate with study groups, project teams, dorm groups, and roommates |
| **Labs** | Complete paid product-improvement tasks for DormWay |

The product works best when these surfaces feel like extensions of the same brain, not disconnected tools.

---

## 7. Mix Is the Multiplayer Layer

Mix turns DormWay into shared, **student-aware** spaces — anchored to a real thing (a room, a class, a project), private to who you invite. It is **not a social network and not a feed**: no followers, no strangers; every Mix leads with the job.

- **Six Mix types:** Coordination, Study, Project, Event, Dorm, Room
- **Find Times** (live) collects availability and ranks meeting times; the link is shareable via a public code so anyone can participate with no account
- **Invites are link/code-based** — a creator generates an invite code, a joiner can join as a guest (email optional) and convert later
- Each Mix can carry claimable tasks, chat, files, a notebook, events, and deadline reminders synced from real course deadlines

<Note>
  **Current production posture (2026-05-30):** Mix is built and live (six types, Find Times, chat, files, notebook, tasks, deadline sync; shipped in iOS v1.0; the `feature.crews_enabled` flag defaults ON). **Joining** is open; **creating** a Mix is currently allowlist-gated.

  **Planned (decided 2026-05-30, DORM-1518, pending build + Riley sign-off):** removing the creator allowlist so any student can start a Mix, an email quick-start for room setup, a standalone `/mix` page, and a **Mix Pro** "coming" tier (cross-term rosters, archiving, org-scale). Not yet live. (Code identifiers remain `crews` / `crew_*`.)
</Note>

## 8. Activation Recovery Is Part of the Product

DormWay treats activation recovery as part of the product system, not someone else's problem:

- onboarding nudges point students toward the next best first action
- Customer.io carries the short-window recovery cadence after signup
- public tools and shareable surfaces reduce the setup students need before they understand the value

So "how DormWay works" now includes acquisition and recovery, not only the steady-state product.

## 9. Platform Coverage

| Platform | Current role |
|----------|--------------|
| **Web** | Full-featured student workspace, including the Today customization system |
| **iPhone** | Fast daily check-ins, realtime updates, widgets, Live Activities, alarms; tabs are Today · Tasks · Schedule · Courses · Mix |
| **iPad** | Higher-density student workspace, plus richer note-taking and study workflows |

The principle is **same brain, different density**. Underlying student state and prioritization logic stay aligned across platforms even when the layout changes.

---

## 10. The Student Experience Should Feel Simpler Than the System Is

Students should experience DormWay as relief, not infrastructure. The complexity lives underneath so the surface can stay honest, calm, and fast. That is the operating standard for every part of the product: **close uncertainty quickly, do not create more of it.**

DormWay is **free for students** at the core (not "free forever").
