# How DormWay Works

DormWay is built around one low-maintenance loop: connect a real school source once, let the system build your semester in the background, then check **Today** in seconds instead of maintaining a planner by hand.

It does not replace Canvas, and it is not asking the student to build a second life-management system from scratch. The job is to merge what school already knows and keep it current.

<Note>
  The trust boundary matters: DormWay uses AI to extract, normalize, and rank school data. It does **not** write essays, do homework, or pretend to know things it cannot verify.
</Note>

## 1. Reach First Value Fast

DormWay's canonical first-value actions are simple:

- **Upload a syllabus**
- **Import a class schedule**
- **Connect Canvas**

Any one of those can unlock first value, because first value means the student can see upcoming deadlines and obligations in one place.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Upload a syllabus" icon="file-arrow-up">
    DormWay extracts deadlines, grading rules, recurring academic events, and term-level risk signals from the PDF.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Import a schedule" icon="calendar-days">
    A PDF, screenshot, or calendar source becomes structured class blocks, course links, and time-aware context.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connect Canvas" icon="link">
    Courses, assignments, announcements, and grade changes start syncing into the same student view.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The key product rule is that onboarding should make at least one of these actions obvious. DormWay should never force a student through confusing branching before they can see something useful.

---

## 2. DormWay Builds the Semester Model

Once a student gives DormWay a real source, the platform starts doing the merge work the student should not have to do manually:

- Deadlines are extracted from syllabi and LMS data
- Schedule events are normalized into a single timeline
- Course contexts are linked to the right term, campus, and student
- Conflicts, peak weeks, and hidden workload get computed in the background
- Source data stays canonical, while student edits can layer on top

This is where DormWay stops being "a planner" and becomes a maintained academic layer. The student adds a source. The product keeps it alive.

---

## 3. StudentWatcher Keeps It Current

Behind the scenes, each enrolled student is backed by a long-running workflow called **StudentWatcher**. Its job is to keep the student's academic state fresh without making them re-enter data all semester.

At a high level, that means:

- A fresh day plan can be generated at **5:00 AM campus local time**
- Calendar and Canvas syncs are evaluated on a recurring cadence
- Important changes can push through in real time
- Context shifts can trigger plan and notification updates
- The student's state survives restarts, retries, and normal infrastructure churn

Students should feel the result of this system, not its complexity. The product ideal is: open the app, glance once, know where you stand.

---

## 4. Today Is the Default Check-In

Today is not meant to be an infinite dashboard or a discovery feed. It exists to answer one question quickly:

**"Am I okay, and what do I need to deal with?"**

That changes the product behavior in a few important ways:

- Priority comes before pure chronology
- Urgent obligations must be visible without hunting for them
- The screen should feel finite and calm, not bottomless
- The default view should work immediately, with customization available but never required
- Anything shown as certain should be source-verifiable

On web, Today already supports a real customization layer: persistent layout, drag-and-drop reordering, widget resizing, hide/restore, and add-widget behavior. The main product issue today is discoverability of that power, not the absence of it.

---

## 5. Students Go Deeper Only When They Need To

Today is the glance surface. The deeper product surfaces exist for the moments when a student needs more detail or wants to act:

| Surface | What students use it for |
|---------|--------------------------|
| **Tasks** | Review assignments and obligations from multiple sources in one place |
| **Schedule** | See classes, time blocks, and calendar events in a normalized timeline |
| **Courses / Syllabus Intelligence** | Understand one course deeply: workload, policies, key dates, red flags |
| **Library / Notes** | Revisit documents, notes, and course materials |
| **Ace** | Ask questions against the student's own courses, schedule, and materials |
| **Crews** | Coordinate with study groups, project teams, or dorm groups |
| **Labs** | Complete paid product-improvement tasks for DormWay |

The product works best when these surfaces feel like extensions of the same brain, not disconnected tools.

---

## 6. Platform Coverage

| Platform | Current role |
|----------|--------------|
| **Web** | Full-featured student workspace, including the Today customization system |
| **iPhone** | Fast daily check-ins, realtime updates, widgets, Live Activities, alarms |
| **iPad** | Higher-density student workspace, plus richer note-taking and study workflows |

The important product principle is **same brain, different density**. The underlying student state and prioritization logic should stay aligned across platforms even when the layout changes.

---

## 7. The Student Experience Should Feel Simpler Than the System Is

Students should experience DormWay as relief, not as infrastructure. The complexity lives underneath so the surface can stay honest, calm, and fast.

That is the operating standard for every part of the product: **close uncertainty quickly, do not create more of it.**
