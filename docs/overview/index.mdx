# DormWay

DormWay is the academic layer that keeps itself current from real school data and turns that into one calm answer: **"Am I okay, and what matters now?"**

The product now has two connected faces:

- an authenticated student workspace built around Today, Schedule, Tasks, Courses, Ace, and Crews
- public and shareable entry surfaces that let students experience value before they fully commit

The through-line is still the same: students should not have to maintain a second planner by hand. DormWay handles the merge, the refresh cycle, and the ranking of what matters.

<Note>
  Public promise: **"Never wonder what's due."**

  Trust boundary: **"We use AI for dates, not essays."**

  Core offering: **free for students** at the core level.
</Note>

## Product Shape

DormWay is not trying to replace school systems or become a generic productivity tool. It sits on top of the systems students already have, merges the pieces they cannot keep straight on their own, and turns that into a calm, finite view of what needs attention.

The current growth thesis matters to the product shape: DormWay increasingly uses shareable, no-auth, or low-friction surfaces to get students into the product with real intent before asking them to configure everything. That is why public tools, guest patterns, and activation recovery now sit next to the core student workspace instead of outside it.

That is why the Overview section is organized around the student loop, the architecture underneath it, and the brand rules that keep the product honest.
