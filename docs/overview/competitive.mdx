# Competitive Positioning

DormWay's two closest category competitors are **Mindgrasp** and **Shovel**. This page distills the May 2026 primary-source teardown of both — how each frames itself, where DormWay legitimately wins, where we honestly do not, and the guardrail that keeps comparison claims trust-safe.

<Note>
  Anything here that becomes on-page copy or a customer-facing comparison claim is **Riley sign-off** (positioning) and must stay expectation-safe. The teardown drives the copy direction for the homepage / Mix marketing initiative (DORM-1518), which is **decided but not yet shipped**.
</Note>

---

## How each competitor frames itself

| | Frame | What it does | Pricing | AI posture |
|---|-------|--------------|---------|------------|
| **Mindgrasp** | "Study Smarter with AI" | Content → study-materials engine: feed it a lecture/PDF/video/link, it generates notes, summaries, flashcards, quizzes, and a 24/7 "AI tutor" | ~$71.88/yr Basic, ~$107.88/yr Scholar; monthly $5.99–$10.99 | Loudly AI-forward — "AI does the studying" |
| **Shovel** | "A Study Planner That Gets You. Grades. Friends. Freedom." | Study-plan **execution**: upload a syllabus or sync an LMS → turns "DUE dates into DO dates," time-blocks the work, "Cushion" forecasts whether you'll finish | $9.79/mo or $35/yr on-site (reviews cite up to ~$200/yr historically) | Planner mechanic, not an AI-content pitch |
| **DormWay** | "Never wonder what's due." / "Every deadline. One timeline." | Always-current, **sourced** truth merged across Canvas + syllabi + schedule + calendar + buried PDFs, layered on a campus + college-town world model — plus **Mix** (people) | Free for students at the core | "AI for your deadlines, not your essays" |

Note the gap each competitor leaves: Mindgrasp organizes the document you paste and never touches deadlines or your LMS; Shovel organizes the tasks from one LMS or one syllabus. Neither carries any campus or world context.

## Where DormWay legitimately wins

<Note>
  These are the seven wedges from the teardown, ordered with the deepest moat first. Wedge #1 is the spine of any "why us" comparison — do not undersell it.
</Note>

1. **Campus + college-town world model (the deepest moat).** Beyond merging *your* sources, DormWay carries a model of your campus and college town: weather, dining, campus + city events, safety / news alerts, transportation & parking, your school's real academic calendar (add/drop, finals, per-campus registrar deadlines), and housing — plus the campus student graph (Country → State → City → Campus → Building → Course → Student). Mindgrasp sees only the document you paste; Shovel sees only the tasks from one LMS. Neither has any campus/world context at all.

2. **Multi-source merge with provenance.** Shovel imports from *one* LMS or *one* syllabus; Mindgrasp doesn't touch deadlines or LMS at all. DormWay reconciles Canvas + every syllabus + schedule + the buried PDF into one **sourced** timeline. Neither competitor does the cross-source merge.

3. **Always-current / self-healing.** Shovel is a planner *you* maintain; Mindgrasp is per-document. DormWay's StudentWatcher keeps syncing and reconciling without re-uploading.

4. **Mix — multiplayer, which neither has.** Shovel literally markets "Friends" / "more time with your friends" but has **no group feature** to deliver it. DormWay can out-execute Shovel's own promise with a real product (shared schedules, Find Times, group deadlines anchored to a class or dorm). Mix is the current product name (renamed from Crews ~2026-04-25); code identifiers remain `crews` / `crew_*`. Mix is **not** a social network or feed.

5. **Free at the core.** Both competitors charge ($35–$200/yr). For the Class of 2030 pre-semester push, "free for students" at the core is a wedge they can't match without torching their revenue model.

6. **We start in high school — a lifecycle neither competitor has.** Both are tools for the *enrolled* mid-grind student. DormWay begins working before college: a high-school senior enrolls as their incoming cohort, picks their campus, and DormWay sets up their academic world before day one — "get ahead before you arrive."

7. **The AI boundary is a credibility asset, not a gap.** Mindgrasp leans into "AI does the studying," which faculty, parents, and many students distrust. "DormWay organizes your school — it doesn't do your work" (AI for your deadlines, not your essays) is a trust position Mindgrasp can't co-opt without abandoning its pitch.

## Where we honestly do NOT win

<Note>
  Do not pretend otherwise. The don't-overclaim guardrail below is positioning canon.
</Note>

- **Mindgrasp beats us on content → study materials** (auto-flashcards, quizzes, auto-notes from a lecture). We deliberately don't do this; a "make me a quiz" student is not our user, and we must not claim it.
- **Shovel's execution mechanic is a sharper single story.** DUE→DO, reading-time estimates, the Cushion forecast — crisp and demoable. Our day plan exists but reads less like a tight one-line promise today.
- **Both have years of narrow, confident positioning.** Our "academic context layer" story is broader, so it can read as *less specific* to a student who just wants "tell me what to do today." This is a copy risk, not a product gap.

---

## Don't-overclaim guardrail

> 🛑 **No "we do everything they do."** We do not beat Mindgrasp at auto-flashcards/quizzes, and Shovel's single-feature execution story is sharper than ours today. Comparison claims must stay expectation-safe and carry Riley sign-off.

Two grounding caveats that any comparison copy must respect:

- **World model — claim the capability, not deep coverage of every campus.** The *structure* spans effectively every US campus and town. Deep LLM enrichment (events / news / safety / logistics) is rolling out target-campus-first — live on pilot campuses, not yet deep on all of them. Claim the capability; don't claim we deeply know every campus yet.
- **Start-in-high-school is built and on-thesis, but nascent.** The pre-semester funnel shipped recently. Frame it as "we start in high school," not "thousands of high schoolers use us."

## How this drives the reframe

The teardown is the input to the homepage / Mix marketing copy direction (PRD §3.5, DORM-1518):

| Wedge | Copy move |
|-------|-----------|
| World model + lifecycle | Lead the whole story here: "DormWay doesn't just organize what you upload — it already knows your campus, your college town, and gets you ahead before day one." |
| AI boundary vs Mindgrasp | Say the boundary louder as a weapon, not a hedge: AI for your deadlines, not your essays. |
| Mix vs Shovel's "friends" | Show the group product Shovel markets but can't deliver: "the planner that actually connects you to your people." |
| Steal Shovel's clarity | Adopt a concrete DUE→DO-style promise in the in-semester hero — but anchor it to the multi-source merge Shovel lacks. |
| Free + pre-semester | Lead with the two things neither competitor can answer. |

<Note>
  Current production posture: today's product runs without the redesigned homepage or the Mix marketing band. The competitive copy direction above is **planned** (decided 2026-05-30, DORM-1518, pending build and Riley sign-off) — not yet live.
</Note>
