# Class of 2030

DormWay's current Class of 2030 push is a pre-arrival activation campaign, not a generic awareness launch. The job is to meet incoming Fall 2026 freshmen at the moments when transition anxiety spikes, then turn that attention into real setup before classes begin.

<Note>
  Current campaign name: **See Your Semester**

  Current density focus: **UMich** and **UCLA** as the main beachheads for Fall 2026 execution.
</Note>

---

## Current Objective

| Topic | Current truth |
|-------|---------------|
| **Audience** | Incoming Fall 2026 freshmen, with density emphasis on UMich and UCLA. |
| **Primary goal** | Reach **10,000+ activated users by the start of September 2026**. |
| **Beachhead targets** | The campaign brief names **1,500+ UMich** users and **1,000+ UCLA** users as the density goals. |
| **Revenue layer** | The same brief targets **500+ Pro conversions** across student monthly and parent lifetime offers. |
| **Budget shape** | Small April paid test first, then a larger August blitz only if the funnel proves out. |
| **Message spine** | "Never wonder what's due." plus seasonal utility, not vague brand awareness. |

The important read is that DormWay is not trying to win freshmen with a single launch week. The plan is to stay relevant from decision season through the first syllabus drop.

---

## Seasonal Sequence

The current Class of 2030 campaign ladders through four distinct moments:

| Phase | Current job | DormWay surface |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **April** | Finals utility | `dormway.app/finals` and the grade-calculator entry point |
| **May-June** | Pre-arrival trust | Decision Day content, course-selection guidance, Discord, and Campus Lead coverage |
| **July** | Social setup | Room Crew, roommate coordination, and creator-led move-in content |
| **August** | Syllabus activation | `syllabus@dormway.app`, paid geo bursts, orientation-week presence, and setup recovery |

This sequence matters because the campaign is designed to feel like one coherent student journey:

- finals help proves immediate utility
- schedule and class-selection moments make DormWay feel relevant before move-in
- roommate and social setup create sharing loops
- syllabus upload becomes the activation payoff once class materials arrive

---

## Product Hooks Inside the Campaign

The growth brief is unusually product-specific. These are the clearest student hooks in the current plan:

| Moment | Product hook | Why it matters |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| **Finals stress** | Grade calculator / finals landing page | DormWay earns trust with a real answer before signup friction. |
| **Course registration** | Semester preview from imported schedules | Students can see the shape of fall before the term starts. |
| **Housing assignments** | Room Crew | Roommate coordination creates organic invites instead of referral chores. |
| **Syllabus drop** | Email-to-ingest at `syllabus@dormway.app` | This is the "holy shit" moment where DormWay turns documents into a usable semester. |
| **Post-activation upsell** | Pro soft launch + parent lifetime page | Pro comes after setup value is clear, not before. |

That last point is important: the Class of 2030 brief treats Pro as a follow-on conversion layer, not the main acquisition message.

---

## Channel Mix

The current campaign uses a simple channel hierarchy:

| Channel type | Current role |
|--------------|--------------|
| **Owned** | Riley-led TikTok/Reels, Instagram, Discord, and Customer.io sequences carry the main narrative. |
| **Earned** | Reddit, Class of 2030 pages, and Campus Leads provide peer trust and local reach. |
| **Paid** | Snapchat and TikTok are the active paid channels, geo-targeted around named beachheads. |

Two rules stay consistent across the source docs:

- Riley's creator voice should sound like a student talking to peers, not a company talking at customers.
- Paid only makes sense when attribution is good enough to connect source, campus, signup, and first meaningful action.

---

## Operating Rules

- Lead with the student problem, not with "AI-powered" claims.
- Treat campus density as the success condition, not broad national awareness.
- Use ambassadors and Campus Leads as genuine users and local connectors, not as a giant field-marketing program.
- Do not move into August-scale spend unless the April test and July readiness checks both pass.
- Keep the public promise simple: **"Never wonder what's due."**

The campaign is best read as DormWay's current freshman operating system, not as a one-off marketing memo.
