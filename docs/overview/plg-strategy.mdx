# PLG Strategy

DormWay's current product-led growth strategy is straightforward: students should be able to encounter a real, useful surface before they ever commit to full setup. The product is not only the place students land after signup. It is the acquisition loop itself.

<Note>
  Current PLG conversion pattern: **public URL -> real value -> natural gate -> signup CTA -> authenticated product**
</Note>

---

## Current PLG Rules

- Public surfaces have to do real work, not act like demos.
- The share moment should be native to student behavior, not bolted on.
- Core student value stays free.
- DormWay should lead with outcomes, not AI mechanics.
- Every surface should support activation quality and campus density, not just traffic.

---

## Current Proof Points

The vault's current PLG docs point to a few patterns that already validate the model:

- **Grade calculator** is the clearest live example of value before signup.
- **Campus and course SEO pages** already expose real school and syllabus intelligence.
- **Guest/shareable patterns** exist in the product and show how DormWay can preview utility before authentication.
- **Activation recovery nudges** are now treated as part of the product system, not separate marketing cleanup.

The immediate PLG job is not inventing an entirely new model. It is extending the value-first pattern into more shareable, higher-frequency student moments.

## Five Approved PLG Surfaces

These are the current approved PLG surfaces for the August welcome-week push.

| Surface | Student job | Current status in the source docs |
|---------|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Semester Card** | Share "what classes are you taking?" as a clean card instead of a screenshot | Approved. OG/image infrastructure exists; feature-specific implementation still needed. |
| **Class Timeline Share** | Share a semester schedule for group-chat coordination | Approved. Schedule rendering exists; public share layer is not yet shipped. |
| **iOS Widgets** | Keep DormWay visible on the lock screen and home screen | Approved. WidgetKit target exists; surface content still needs implementation. |
| **Find a Time** | Let groups answer "when are we all free?" with a public link | Approved. Backend is described as complete; public frontend and gateway routes still need shipping. |
| **Founding Card** | Give early users a collectible identity surface they can share | Approved in strategy; not found in the codebase audit yet. |

The strategy note is explicit that these are **approved surfaces**, not a claim that all five are already live.

## Freshman Event Spine

The PLG work is now easiest to read as a sequence of student moments:

| Event | Why it matters | PLG surfaces most tied to it |
|-------|----------------|------------------------------|
| **Decision Day** | Students are choosing a school and forming their first college questions. | Campaign landing pages, finals utility, early trust content, referral capture. |
| **Schedules** | Students want to know what fall will look like before they arrive. | Semester Card, Class Timeline Share, Find a Time. |
| **Roommates** | Students are coordinating with the people they will live with. | Room Crew, Discord/community surfaces, invite flows. |
| **Move-in** | Phones are visible, identity signaling matters, and social sharing spikes. | Founding Card, iOS Widgets, home-feel surfaces. |
| **Orientation** | Students are setting up accounts and deciding what tools stay on their phone. | Announcement banner, changelog, founder voice, bug/feedback paths. |
| **Class start** | Real assignments and schedules start to matter. | Syllabus activation, Semester Card, Class Timeline Share, Find a Time, activation recovery. |

The approved surfaces are the product layer under this event spine. They are not isolated features; they are the shareable responses to these moments.

## Current build posture

The current PLG backlog is grouped around a few concrete workstreams:

- **Foundation:** flags, analytics, moderation, privacy/legal, launch gating.
- **Semester Card:** first shareable planning artifact.
- **Class Timeline Share:** class-level schedule sharing for group chats.
- **iOS Widgets:** lock-screen and home-screen visibility.
- **Find a Time:** group coordination link for availability.
- **Founding Card:** collectible identity and social proof.
- **Launch readiness:** rollout, QA, enablement.

There is also a smaller set of support work in Linear that makes the surfaces feel alive in-product: announcement banner, changelog, founder voice, Discord, feedback/bug reporting, home-feel polish, and activation recovery.

---

## What PLG Is Not

The current vault is unusually clear about the anti-goals:

- **Not gamification.** No streaks, points, or leaderboards.
- **Not a huge ambassador machine.** Campus Leads matter, but DormWay is not trying to build a 100+ ambassador program.
- **Not a freemium wall around the core student product.** Core student acquisition and activation should stay free.
- **Not fake preview pages.** The surface itself should be a useful artifact.

That matters because it keeps DormWay's growth model tied to trust and actual student utility instead of novelty tactics.

## Rollout Prerequisites

The PLG thesis and engineering audit both call out a small set of prerequisites before DormWay should push harder on public surfaces:

| Area | Current truth |
|------|---------------|
| **Feature flags** | Ready. Hypertune, `system_config`, and campus-level flags already exist for controlled rollout and kill switches. |
| **Instrumentation** | Core events exist for signup platform and landing uploads, but the GTM docs still treat attribution reliability as a scale gate that must be verified continuously. |
| **Lifecycle follow-up** | Customer.io integration exists and can support activation recovery, but some PLG-specific traits still need wiring. |
| **Content moderation** | Needed for any public surface that accepts free text. |
| **Policy/legal** | The PLG thesis flags a privacy-policy issue for under-18 users in the Class of 2030 funnel. |

---

## Current Execution Posture

DormWay's current PLG posture is intentionally narrow:

- extend the proven value-first pattern rather than multiplying half-finished public tools
- ship a small number of shareable surfaces that create authentic student distribution
- measure them against activation and density, not vanity traffic
- keep the main product promise intact: **"Never wonder what's due."**

The core idea is that DormWay grows best when students share something genuinely helpful, not when they are handed a referral chore.
