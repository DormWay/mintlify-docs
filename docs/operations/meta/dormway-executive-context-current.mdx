# DormWay Executive Context

Entry-point doc for any agent or subagent working on DormWay.

## What DormWay Is

Crisis prevention infrastructure for college students. DormWay aggregates syllabi, Canvas, and calendar into one always-current view so students never wonder what's due.

Core promise: **"Never wonder what's due."**

## What DormWay Is Not

- Not a productivity app or planner
- Not an AI chatbot company
- Not a social network for students
- Not a generic EdTech platform
- Not trying to replace Canvas or university systems

## Current Company State (as of March 2026)

- Two-person founding team through at least August 2026
- **Riley** (22, student at Michigan): product direction, messaging, branding, marketing, GTM, UI/UX
- **Ethan**: backend, infrastructure, technical architecture, analytics, operational readiness, scalability
- Pre-seed stage, targeting seed raise with lead path by mid-May 2026, close by June 2026

## Active Target

Class of 2030 — incoming Michigan freshmen as the primary wedge. Not a second product line; a focused entry surface that bridges into the main academic workflow.

## Operating Philosophy

- Quality over quantity until activation and retention are trustworthy
- Dense campus adoption over broad shallow acquisition
- Trust, clarity, and first value over feature sprawl or AI hype
- Plans must be operable by two people
- Short-form content is a feeder, not the main engine

## Primary Metrics

- WFAU (weekly first-action users)
- Activation
- D7, D14, W4 retention

For canonical metric definitions, see [GTM Playbook - Spring 2026 (Current)](/docs/product/go-to-market/gtm-playbook-spring-2026-current).

## Decision Filter

Before starting any new work, ask:

1. Does this improve first value?
2. Does this improve trust?
3. Does this improve retention?
4. Does this increase freshman density in a target cohort?
5. Can Riley and Ethan realistically operate it through August?
