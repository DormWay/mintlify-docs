# Fundraising Narrative

Canonical investor story for decks, outreach, meeting prep, and diligence support.

## What DormWay Is

Crisis prevention infrastructure for college students. One place to see everything that's due, always current, across all classes. Upload a syllabus or connect Canvas — the entire semester timeline builds automatically.

Core promise: "Never wonder what's due."

## Why Now

College students operate across fragmented systems — Canvas, syllabi, email, calendars — with no unified view. The 3am "did I forget something?" panic is universal. LMS platforms serve institutions, not students. No one owns the student's academic clarity layer.

## Why Riley and Ethan

- **Riley** (22, student at University of Michigan): Built DormWay because he needed it to survive his own coursework. Still in classes, still using the product daily. Owns product, brand, and GTM.
- **Ethan**: Technical co-founder. Owns backend, infrastructure, analytics, and operational readiness. Builds systems that can scale.

Founder-market fit: Riley has lived the problem. The best-performing growth content comes from his authentic student experience.

## What Proof Matters at This Stage

- Activation quality (students getting real value, not just signing up)
- Weekly retention (D7, D14, W4)
- Dense campus adoption in a target cohort
- Repeatable first-value loop
- Real student love — qualitative and quantitative

## What DormWay Should Raise On

- Strong user love
- Trusted retention in a dense freshman cohort
- Clear founder-market fit
- Strong product quality relative to stage
- A credible path to campus-by-campus expansion
- Evidence that the freshman wedge is a repeatable distribution advantage

## What DormWay Should Not Try To Raise On

- Inflated top-of-funnel numbers
- Messy activation
- Contradictory monetization story
- Too many unfinished growth programs
