# Cost Optimization Strategy

Cost optimization strategy for third-party infrastructure and platform services.

## Input Inventory

Primary source: [Third-Party Tools & Services Inventory](/docs/engineering/architecture/third-party-tools-services-inventory).

## Optimization Priorities

1. AI/LLM spend: enforce model routing and fallbacks via PortKey policy.
2. Infrastructure spend: review cache hit rates, storage retention, and over-provisioned services.
3. Communication spend: reduce noisy messaging/event volume and optimize targeting.
4. Tooling spend: prefer open-source/default tiers where enterprise upgrades are not justified.

## Cadence

- Run monthly service review for usage, limits, and pricing changes.
- Track projected savings vs observed invoice deltas by category.
- Record migration risk before changing critical providers.

## Guardrails

- Do not reduce reliability/SLA for student-critical paths.
- Keep fallback paths documented before aggressive vendor consolidation.
- Tie cost controls to explicit product and reliability outcomes.
