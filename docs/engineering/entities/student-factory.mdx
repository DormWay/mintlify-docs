
Get students currently located in a city (based on recent GPS location data)
Uses PostGIS spatial queries to find students whose GPS coordinates are near
any campus in the target city.

This is for traveling students - students whose home campus is NOT in this city,
but their recent GPS data shows they are physically near a campus in this city.
